# Region sheets

Regions are designed after the cosmology and powers, but before individual settlements. A region is not a biome label: it must explain why a power can hold ground there and what the player can do there that cannot happen elsewhere.

## Sheet

1. **Identity.** One sentence a player can repeat.
2. **Land.** Terrain, weather, resources and the practical constraint they impose.
3. **Holder.** Who governs it, how they maintain that claim, and who resists.
4. **Daily life.** Work, trade, food, law, visual character and an ordinary local problem.
5. **Pressure.** The active conflict or approaching change.
6. **Secrets.** Believed / suspected / true-or-sealed, following `../universe.md`.
7. **Play.** Skills, combat styles, creatures, materials, landmarks, bosses and quest hooks.
8. **Clock.** What changes if the player does nothing.

## Initial coverage

The initial sheets cover territorial powers and the places where their conflicts overlap. Their names remain provisional unless already marked as kept in `../names.md`.
