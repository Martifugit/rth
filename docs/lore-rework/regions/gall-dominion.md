# Gall's dominion: the furnace country

**Status:** proposed regional sheet. The region's formal name remains open; **Scoria** is proposed for its capital.

## Identity

An industrial volcanic country where useful change and catastrophic change are separated by rank, law and a thin wall.

## Land and holder

Ash slopes, lava-cut valleys, cinder fields and heat-fed terraces make ordinary agriculture difficult but support furnace craft on an unmatched scale. House Gall rules from fortified works, controls water and vent access, and keeps towns dependent on its heat, medicines and employment.

## Daily life

People live by shifts, bell codes and fire regulations. A furnace closure means hunger; a successful working means work, goods and prestige. Gall's inspectors are feared because they can condemn a workshop as unsafe, heretical or strategically necessary with the same stamp.

## Pressure and clock

The empire's siege line forces Gall to increase output while the Athanor's upper vents become less stable. If unchecked, Gall chooses production over containment: a working quarter becomes permanently altered, the army receives better weapons, and the displaced are sent toward the frontier.

## Play

Ash storms, foundries, glassworks, fire-resistant creatures, siege works and volatile-resource choices. Occultism should feel productive here before the player sees its full cost.
