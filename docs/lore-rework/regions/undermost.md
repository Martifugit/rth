# Undermost: the old deep

**Status:** proposed regional sheet; Undermost is a retained name.

## Identity

The world beneath the world, where the oldest inhabitants are guarding something they will not name.

## Land and holder

Undermost is not one cavern kingdom but a layered depth of drowned shafts, abandoned works, subterranean rivers and places changed by old revisions. Its older peoples hold routes through knowledge, sealed gates and the fact that surface armies cannot provision themselves there.

## Pressure and clock

The Athanor's lower seals weaken; Gall approaches from above and the dwarves seal routes from the side. The older peoples start evacuating one depth into another. If nobody asks why, the first surface town above a breach changes overnight.

## Play

Deep navigation, excavation, ancient revision strata, sealed cultures, altered matter and the bound-thing mystery. Riddance should be reachable only from exceptionally dangerous seams here.
