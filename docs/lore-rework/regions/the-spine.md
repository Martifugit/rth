# The Spine: Reckoner highlands and dwarven deep

**Status:** proposed regional sheet. "The Spine" is intentionally a descriptive common name.

## Identity

A neutral mountain road built over a secret neither side can afford to expose.

## Land and holder

Passes, high basins, observatories, beacon towers and terrace towns belong to the Reckoners. Mines, shafts and old sealed works belong to the dwarves. The Concord keeps each out of the other's domain in public; in practice both conduct quiet expeditions below the treaty line.

## Pressure and clock

Gall drives a furnace-line toward a lower vent while an impossible moonfall prediction threatens a major pass. If ignored, the pass closes, trade fails and imperial troops are invited in as relief.

## Play

Ranged combat, climbing routes, weather systems, cart-lines, observatory puzzles and deep excavations. Frostpeak Forge and Lucern Pass are natural anchor locations.
