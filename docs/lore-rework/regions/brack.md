# Brack: the broken coast of the lesser houses

**Status:** proposed regional sheet; Brack is the recommended retained land-name.

## Identity

Salt, secrets and sea caves: the place the great powers can raid but cannot comfortably rule.

## Land and holder

Tidal fens, salt marsh, sea stacks and flooded caves fracture authority. The lesser houses survive in defensible pockets and hidden cells, selling rare salts, altered creatures and dangerous services to every side. Skerrin is their contested meeting place; Scalth's pans turn brine and worse into valuable salts.

## Pressure and clock

Gall wants submission; the empire wants purges; local houses want neither. A black-salt seep from a new Athanor leak poisons the marsh while creating the most profitable Scalth harvest in generations. If untouched, the houses turn the crisis into a short civil war and the coast becomes unusable.

## Play

Occultism, tides, contraband, salt works, vitriol mites, shifting routes and alliances that change with the water.
