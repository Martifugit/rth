# Sere: the country of guarded water

**Status:** proposed regional sheet; Sere is retained canon.

## Identity

A dry kingdom that survives because it knows where water is and when not to share it.

## Land and holder

Sere's wells, qanat-like channels, saltfig groves and caravan roads cannot support an occupying army for long. Local water law, engineers and Queen Saffiya's authority turn scarcity into independence, but the empire's river salient is a hand around its throat.

## Pressure and clock

The salient begins surveying an upstream diversion while Gall offers weapons and an alliance. If ignored, Sere must choose a protector and loses the neutral trade role that has kept it alive.

## Play

Water logistics, desert travel, buried channels, caravan politics and a tension between practical survival and anti-imperial resistance.
