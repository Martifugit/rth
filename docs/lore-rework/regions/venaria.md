# Venaria: the heartland of Cruor's empire

**Status:** proposed regional sheet; Venaria is a retained name.

## Identity

The country that turns blood, grain and promises into empire.

## Land and holder

Wide river valleys, managed floodplains, roads, mills and dense market towns make Venaria the empire's logistical heart. The Lien holds it through monuments, courts, garrisons and the promise that a registered oath will be enforced.

## Daily life

People farm, mill, tally and travel. A monument is the centre of a town's calendar: collection day, legal day and festival day are deliberately the same day. The system offers genuine safety, while making refusal visible and expensive.

## Pressure and clock

Rising blood quotas provoke smuggling, forged pledge records and Faceless recruitment. If nobody acts, the Lien imposes a new census that turns private resistance into open rebellion.

## Play

Roadside shrines, granaries, oath courts, river barges, tax convoys and salients that interrupt an otherwise settled landscape. Melee, Religion and civic investigation belong here.
