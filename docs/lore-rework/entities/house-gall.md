# House Gall

**Status:** proposed sheet. House Gall is the decided dominant occult house; its source is proposed here as the Athanor.

## Origin

Gall began around a hot breach in the deep earth, where miners found that bile, ore and living tissue did not keep their accustomed forms. Its founders learned to draw change from the breach, then burned rivals who called the practice a contagion. It became the only house willing to rule in the open.

## Belief

Gall holds that the world is not sacred because it is unfinished. Every fixed form is a temporary claim, and the person willing to alter first survives the next Turn. Other houses call this arrogance; Gall calls it honesty.

## Ground

Gall holds volcanic country around **Scoria** (proposed name), fortified foundries, ash farms and the upper vents of the Athanor. Its terrain makes invasion costly, while its alchemical furnaces provide weapons, medicines, pigments, glass and disasters that its neighbours still buy.

## How it lives

House rank is earned through successful workings rather than inherited cleanly. Every workshop is part laboratory, part shrine and part arsenal. Ordinary people accept strict fire law, curfews and inspection because Gall's practical magic makes harsh land productive. Failed workings are hidden, exiled, or sold to lesser houses.

## Wants and weakness

Gall wants every occult house under a single law and access to the deeper Athanor chambers beneath the range. It fears that the dwarves know the breach's true purpose, and that its celebrated control is only the shallowest use of something much older.

## Secret

Gall's highest masters do not command the Athanor. They keep its vents from closing. The larger their workings become, the more the source must be fed with matter, memories or living humours.

## Clock

Gall expands a supposedly defensive furnace-line toward the mountains. The empire answers with sieges; the dwarves sabotage both sides. If no one intervenes, Gall opens a lower vent and begins a regional transformation that neither army can occupy.

## In play

- Fire, bile, transmutation, unstable creatures, industrial ruins and contested furnace towns.
- Quest choices can trade short-term material abundance against long-term alteration of a place.
- Gall's front produces boss encounters that change with the local war state.
