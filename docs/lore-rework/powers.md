# The board

The powers of the world: what each holds, why it can hold it, where borders press and what each
wants from the others. The map is redrawn from this. Shares are of the land, rough, and for the
first build; frontiers add land later.

## Territorial powers (proposed)

| Power | Share | Ground, and why it can hold it | Presses on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cruor's empire** | ~30-35% | The rich heart of the continent: rivers, farmland, roads, and features of its own. The largest army, soldiers sworn in blood and made strong by p-sworn. Its border is ragged: **salients** into every neighbour, each a disputed place whose content has states. | Everyone. It must keep swearing in new peoples (p-sworn starves without new oaths) and collecting blood (p-sworn bleeds). |
| **House Gall** (the dominant occult house) | ~15% | A kingdom of its own, openly occult: fire, loud, burns first. *Where: decided with the map.* | The empire, head on. |
| **The lesser occult houses** | ~10% together | Scattered: fens, sea caves, broken country nobody else wants, and hidden cells inside the empire's own cities. They hold ground the empire can burn but not keep. | Each other; the dominant house wants them in line. |
| **The range: p-rationalists on the surface, dwarves inside, under a treaty** | ~15% of the surface, and most of what lies under it | A long mountain **spine** through the continent, with land on both sides, running from one biome **north into the cold**, where **Frostpeak Forge** is. Meridian need not be by the sea any more. The surface people hold the passes and high ground; the dwarves hold the caves. The treaty's hidden reason: *open*. | Nobody, on purpose. The empire's salient climbs into their foothills. |
| **Sere** | ~10% | Dry country where only its people know the water. Armies that march in die of thirst. | The empire's salient up its river; its own thirst. |
| **Frontiers and wild land** | ~15% | Unclaimed: where the world grows next, and where frontier peoples live. | |

## Geography (decided)

- **No generic lowlands.** The whole continent has geographic features and points of interest
  throughout. The spine is the most exaggerated of them, not the only one.
- **The spine** runs from one biome north into the cold; Frostpeak Forge is up there.
- **Shares are a guide, not a target**: the continent can grow, so the balance can shift.
- **House Gall dominates** the occult houses.

## Relations (proposed)

| Between | Relation |
| --- | --- |
| Empire and Gall | **Open war** along a long front. Neither can win; each claims the other's border towns, which change hands. |
| Empire and the lesser houses | Hunts them and burns their cells. Some imperial lords quietly buy what they make. |
| Empire and the surface people | Needs their passes and roads, and pays their tolls. They won't swear oaths, so the empire can't absorb them peacefully. Its salient climbs into their foothills. |
| Empire and the dwarves | Trade: grain for metal. The dwarves won't swear either, and the empire wants their mines. |
| Empire and Sere | The salient up Sere's river is a hand on its water. Sere pays the empire to keep it from closing. |
| Empire and the Faceless | Hunts them as the Traitor's people. They hollow it out from inside. |
| Gall and the lesser houses | Wants them under its rule. Some submit; some hide from Gall as hard as from the empire. |
| Gall and the surface people | Buys what they sell. They sell to both sides, and profit from the war. |
| Gall and the dwarves | The dwarves keep Gall's fire away from the deep, and won't say why. |
| Gall and Sere | Offers alliance against the empire. Sere hedges. |
| The surface people and the dwarves | The treaty: the surface to one, the caves to the other, no questions either way. Both break the last clause. |
| The surface people and Sere | Sere's caravans cross the range by their passes. |
| The dwarves and Sere | Sere buys dwarven tools to dig for water. |
| The Faceless and the rest | Hidden among all of them. Nobody knows how many. |
