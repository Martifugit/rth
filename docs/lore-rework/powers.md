# The board

The powers of the world: what each holds, why it can hold it, where borders press and what each
wants from the others. The map is redrawn from this. Shares are of the land, rough, and for the
first build; frontiers add land later.

## Territorial powers (proposed)

| Power | Share | Ground, and why it can hold it | Presses on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cruor's empire** | ~30-35% | The rich central lowlands: rivers, farmland, roads. The largest army, soldiers sworn in blood and made strong by p-sworn. Its border is ragged: **salients** into every neighbour, each a disputed place whose content has states. | Everyone. It must keep swearing in new peoples (p-sworn starves without new oaths) and collecting blood (p-sworn bleeds). |
| **The dominant occult house** | ~15% | A kingdom of its own, openly occult. *Which house and where: open (below).* | The empire, head on. |
| **The lesser occult houses** | ~10% together | Scattered: fens, sea caves, broken country nobody else wants, and hidden cells inside the empire's own cities. They hold ground the empire can burn but not keep. | Each other; the dominant house wants them in line. |
| **The range: p-rationalists on the surface, dwarves inside, under a treaty** | ~15% of the surface, and most of what lies under it | A long mountain range. The surface people hold the passes and high ground; the dwarves hold the caves. The treaty's hidden reason: *open*. | Nobody, on purpose. The empire's salient climbs into their foothills. |
| **Sere** | ~10% | Dry country where only its people know the water. Armies that march in die of thirst. | The empire's salient up its river; its own thirst. |
| **Frontiers and wild land** | ~15% | Unclaimed: where the world grows next, and where frontier peoples live. | |
