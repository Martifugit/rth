# occultism

## Intent

Occultism is a combat style which comes out of the occult houses. Everyone has
[bile](../systems/bile.md), a balance between yellow (offensive) and black (defensive); occultists
are the ones who tip it on purpose. Every occult ability pushes bile one way or the other, and the
strongest need a deep lean. Leaning further makes you stronger and also more exposed, so the player
should feel they are walking an edge they chose, deciding in each fight how far to go.

Like every combat style, it supplies itself. Occultists make their own fuel, so nobody depends on a
player economy.

## How it feels to train

- **In a fight** (the main source of XP): your active spell from a [spellbook](../groups/spellbooks.md)
  is your auto-attack, and each cast burns reagents. Builders raise [phlegm](../systems/phlegm.md)
  and push bile. Spenders and ultimates cost phlegm, and some need bile at a certain lean.
- **Leaning has two sides:** each pole gives a buff and a debuff together, so going further is a
  gamble. Out of combat, bile drifts back to zero.
- **Between fights:** the rites of [Distillation](../activities/distillation.md) at a house's site
  make the reagents. They give XP about a third as fast as combat, mostly hands-off, with
  disturbances that reward tending.

## What it unlocks

- **Spellbooks, one per occult house.** No house locks you out. You can learn from several and swap
  between them. A spell is learned once, from a quest or a house contact, and kept forever.
- **House favour:** a house's higher spells and rites ask for its favour as well as a level, so
  you have reasons to travel to new houses and sites.
