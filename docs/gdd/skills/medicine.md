# Medicine

## Intent

Medicine turns herbs and extracts into potions: [phlegm](../systems/phlegm.md),
[bile](../systems/bile.md), boosts and cures. The herbs come from [foraging](foraging.md), and its
potions heal [blood](../systems/blood.md) over time.
