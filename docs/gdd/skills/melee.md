# Melee

## Intent

Melee is close fighting with blades and every other weapon in reach. It is a [combat
style](../systems/combat-style.md) that pays [blood](../systems/blood.md) for power, so the fighter
chooses how much of their own life to spend. [Smithing](smithing.md) makes its weapons and plate.
