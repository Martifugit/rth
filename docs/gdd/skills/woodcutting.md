# Woodcutting

## Intent

Woodcutting fells [trees](../groups/trees.md) for [logs](../groups/logs.md): wood for bows, bolts,
fires and casings, and charcoal for the forge. It needs a [hatchet](../groups/hatchets.md).

## How it feels to train

Mostly calm: swinging at a [tree](../groups/trees.md) for logs and XP.
[Wildfires](../activities/wildfires.md) is the fast, active way to train it.
