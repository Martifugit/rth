# Butchering

## Intent

Butchering turns what the player kills into things to use. A carcass is taken apart at the right [station](../objects/butchering-station.md) with the right tool, and a higher level gets more out of each one. It links fighting to [cooking](cooking.md) and [tailoring](tailoring.md).
