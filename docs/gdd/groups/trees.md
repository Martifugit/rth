# Trees

## What unites them

## Members

Most, but not all trees, can be chopped down through the [Woodcutting](woodcutting.md) skill.
Certain trees may be interacted with in other ways, such as tying a [Rope](rope.md) to them. Trees
that can be chopped down differ in the woodcutting level required to to chop at them.
