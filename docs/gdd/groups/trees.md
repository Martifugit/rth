# Trees

## What unites them

Trees are where [logs](logs.md) come from, so every fire, plank and charcoal bar starts with someone
at a tree with a [hatchet](hatchets.md). Most trees can be chopped, which trains
[Woodcutting](../skills/woodcutting.md). A tree that's cut down leaves a stump and grows back after
a while, for everyone. Trees stand in groups, and the trees in a group can need
[tending](../systems/tree-tending.md). Some trees are used in other ways, such as tying a
[rope](../items/rope.md) to them.

## Members

Trees that can be chopped differ in the Woodcutting level they need, the log they give, how often a
chop fells them, and the land they grow on. Each tree's own page holds its numbers.
