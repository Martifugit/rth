# Occult Spellbooks

## What unites them

Each [occult house](occult-houses.md) has one spellbook. No house locks a player out: they can
learn from several and swap between them. A spell is learned once, from a quest or a house contact,
and kept forever. The active spell is the player's [auto-attack](../systems/auto-attack.md) in
[Occultism](../skills/occultism.md).
