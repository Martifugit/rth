# Nivil

## Intent and mood

The cold north, which nobody claims: moors with granite tors, bogs and standing stones, then pine
forest, then tundra and snow down to a coast of fjords. There are few people and long empty views,
and trappers' camps. It is where the world feels widest and least watched.

## Where it is

North of [Venaria](venaria.md) and [Brack](brack.md). The frozen end of [the Chine](the-chine.md)
comes down on its west, its glaciers reaching the northern sea. The frontier lies beyond it.

## Connections

- [Venaria](venaria.md): the climb from the northern farms onto the moors.
- [The Chine](the-chine.md): the frozen end of the range, past [Frostpeak
  Forge](frostpeak-forge.md).
- [Brack](brack.md): the northern meres.
