# Brack

## Intent and mood

The land of the lesser [occult houses](../groups/occult-houses.md): low, wet and broken, a maze of
meres, channels and reed beds, ending where the sea has eaten the coast into caves and stacks. The
houses hold the dry holms, with causeways between them. It should feel hidden: every hold is on an
island, and someone watches every way in.

## Where it is

The continent's north-east, between [Venaria](venaria.md)'s fields and the sea, with
[Nivil](nivil.md) to the north.

## Connections

- [Venaria](venaria.md): the causeway from the empire's walled port at the great river's mouth, and
  the fens' edge, where the fields give out.
- [Nivil](nivil.md): the northern meres, running up into the cold.
