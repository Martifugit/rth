# Venaria

## Intent and mood

The heart of [Cruor](cruor.md)'s empire: green, wet and settled, its rivers and roads all running to
Cruor on its hill like veins to a heart. Every player starts here, in [Tolling](tolling.md), and
spends the early game here. It should feel safe and well kept, with the empire's blood oath in every
town, toll and monument.

## Where it is

The middle of the continent, east of [the Chine](the-chine.md). [Nivil](nivil.md) lies north,
[Brack](brack.md) north-east, and [Gall](gall.md) south-east, above [the Scarp](the-scarp.md).
[Sere](sere.md) lies south-west, where the green dries out just south of Tolling.

## Connections

- [The Chine](the-chine.md): the salt pass road west of Tolling, and the foothills road up to
  [Meridian](meridian.md).
- [Sere](sere.md): the Sere road south from Tolling, and Sere's river, past the empire's sluice.
- [Gall](gall.md): the few ramps up [the Scarp](the-scarp.md), each one a front.
- [Brack](brack.md): the causeway from the empire's walled port at the great river's mouth.
- [Nivil](nivil.md): the climb from the northern farms onto the moors.
