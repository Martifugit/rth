# Sere

## Intent and mood

A dry kingdom where only its own people know the water: armies that march in die of thirst.

## Where it is

In [the Chine](the-chine.md)'s rain shadow, ruled from [Cistern](cistern.md), built on the deepest
wells.

## Connections

- [Venaria](venaria.md): the Sere road north to [Tolling](tolling.md), and Sere's river, whose upper
  course and sluice the empire holds.
- [The Chine](the-chine.md): the badlands under its southern end, by the dwarves' southern hold.
