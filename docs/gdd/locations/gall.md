# Gall

## Intent and mood

The kingdom of [House Gall](../groups/house-gall.md), the strongest of the [occult
houses](../groups/occult-houses.md): a raised volcanic table of grey ash plains, lava fields and
three volcanoes, one still making new land where its lava meets the sea. It is hot, grey and
hostile, and its fires are seen from the empire at night. Gall and [Cruor](cruor.md)'s empire are at
open war along its edge, and neither can win.

## Where it is

The continent's south-east, lifted above [Venaria](venaria.md) by [the Scarp](the-scarp.md).
[Sere](sere.md) lies west; the sea bounds it south and east. Its city is
[p-gall-seat](p-gall-seat.md), on the great cone's flank.

## Connections

- [Venaria](venaria.md): the few ramps up [the Scarp](the-scarp.md), each one a front.
