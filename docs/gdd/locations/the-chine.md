# The Chine

## Intent and mood

The mountain range that runs the whole height of the continent, always on the western skyline: red
and dry in the south, glaciers in the north. Its surface belongs to
[p-surface-people](../groups/p-surface-people.md), who refuse to serve any god; its caves belong to
the [dwarves](../groups/dwarves.md); [Undermost](undermost.md) lies beneath them. It should feel
high, cold and measured, with nothing in it worshipped. [Meridian](meridian.md),
[Selenfall](selenfall.md) and [Frostpeak Forge](frostpeak-forge.md) are here.

## Where it is

Along the continent's west. Its east face is broad and gentle, falling to [Venaria](venaria.md) and
[Sere](sere.md); its west face is sheer, above the frontier. Its frozen northern end reaches
[Nivil](nivil.md).

## Connections

- [Venaria](venaria.md): the salt pass road from [Tolling](tolling.md), the foothills road to
  Meridian, and the road up to Selenfall.
- [Sere](sere.md): the badlands under its southern end, by the dwarves' southern hold.
- [Nivil](nivil.md): its frozen end, past Frostpeak Forge.
