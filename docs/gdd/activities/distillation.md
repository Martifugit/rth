# Distillation

## Intent

Distillation is how occultists make their own [reagents](../groups/reagents.md) between fights:
mostly hands-off, with disturbances that reward tending.

## How it works

The rites of Distillation turn ingredients into reagents and give
[Occultism](../skills/occultism.md) XP.

## How it starts

At an [occult house's](../groups/occult-houses.md) site.

## Tunables

| Value | Number | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| XP rate | about a third of combat's | Keeps fighting the main way to train Occultism |
