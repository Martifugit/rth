# Concept

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/lore.md` (I, VI), `docs/systems.md` and
`docs/skills.md`.*

R'th is a private online RPG for an invited group of friends. It plays like RuneScape 3 and looks
like Old School RuneScape, in a world of its own: a continent of rival powers, built around the four
humours of old medicine, in a world that was made but never finished. Its makers revise it every few
ages, adding, moving and taking things out; the last revision ended about a thousand years ago, and
the game begins as the next one stirs.

## The experience

You open your eyes in a cold vault under a bell tower, on a bier, under a shroud. The candles are
nearly out. Up the stairs, the bell-ringer stares at you: last night the fishers pulled a body out
of the mere, the bell rang for it, and the church's ledger has you down as dead. Nobody in Tolling
knows who you are. You don't either.

The clerk hands back what was found on you. The apothecary looks you over far more closely than an
apothecary should. A woman by the pond, asleep on her feet, says something only the dead should
know. You can follow that thread, or ignore it and walk out to mine copper, net perch in the mere and
skin rats in the reeds; nothing makes you choose.

Out on the roads the world opens up. East along the lake is Cruor, the empire's capital on its hill,
where knights swear in blood to a god that keeps every promise to the letter. West, the mountains of
the spine, where people who refuse to serve any god measure the sky. South, the dry kingdom of Sere,
which lives because only it knows where the water is. Beyond the Scarp, the volcanic country of the
occult House Gall burns.

You fight with your own body: blood to lose, phlegm that builds as you fight, and bile that you push
hot or cold for power. You gather, butcher, smith and brew everything your fights need, because there
is no market to buy them from. And everywhere you go, the world is a little wrong: a place that isn't
where the old maps put it, a road that ends at nothing, a fish with its eyes on the wrong side.
