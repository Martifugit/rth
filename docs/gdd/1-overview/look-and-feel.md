# Look and feel

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/interface.md`, `docs/world-plan.md` 7 and the owner's
look decisions (2026-10-26). How it is made is the art bible: [VIII](../8-art.md).*

## The world

- **Old School RuneScape's look, R'th's own forms.** Low-poly models, chunky flat colours and small
  point-filtered textures, the classic layout. OSRS references set the technique, never the shapes.
- **Dark and weathered.** Muted, slightly cool ground and water; olive grass in broad patches of
  lighter and darker, yellower and bluer, never per-tile speckle; matte ground under an overcast
  light.
- **Ground painted the RuneScape way:** blended land underneath, crisp made surfaces on top (roads,
  paving, planks) with corners cut on the diagonal, so a road bends at 45 degrees instead of stepping.
- **Paths, not highways.** Plain earth paths with gravel scattered unevenly over them, wandering in
  easy bends. Paving is for towns.
- **Water reads as water:** one flat surface, shores curving down under it, shallows and flow.
- **Each power builds its own roads,** so the road tells you whose land you are in: the empire's
  gravel, Sere's cairned caravan tracks, the spine's switchbacks with a toll at every crossing,
  Gall's black cinder, Brack's causeways and plank walks.
- **Sparse at RuneScape's scale.** Few trees, few buildings, a city of about 30 buildings. Nothing
  fully blocks the camera.

## The interface

A modern dark-fantasy interface over an old-school world: near-black charcoal-brown panels in thin
bronze frames with small gold corners, Cinzel for titles and Alegreya Sans for reading, icons as
tinted silhouettes. Movable windows that dock and tab together. See [V](../5-interface/index.md).

## The dead

The realm of the dead is near-black water and ash, blue-green, lit by candles and soul lanterns; the
dead are drawn glowing and see-through.
