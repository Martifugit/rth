# Feature set

*Draft (2026-11-03). Only what sets R'th apart (Ryan: good graphics and sound are assumed). Built
means it is in the game today; designed means decided but not built.*

| Feature | What the player gets | State | Page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The humours in combat** | Blood (life), phlegm (adrenaline, every style), bile (occultism's axis from black to yellow), devotion (hymns) | Built | [humours](../2-gameplay/humours.md) |
| **Three styles with their own engines** | Occultism's bile, melee's blood-paid verses, ranged's meter (designed, not built) | Occultism and melee built | [combat](../2-gameplay/combat.md) |
| **One faith, two practices** | Religion: hymns any style can sing, verses for melee, monuments that cut you open for pledges | Built | [Religion](../2-gameplay/religion.md) |
| **Sixteen self-sufficient skills** | Every fight's supplies made by your own skills, ten tiers each | Most loops built to tier 3 | [skills](../2-gameplay/skills/index.md) |
| **The carcass at the centre** | Creatures drop carcasses; butchering them gives meat, hide, glands, remains | Built for two families | [Butchering](../2-gameplay/skills/butchering.md) |
| **Skilling that works with the place** | Fire-setting a rock face, tending a coppice, a forge everyone keeps roaring, reading the water | Built | [skills](../2-gameplay/skills/index.md) |
| **Trophy angling** | Find a species' trophy spot, rig your own tackle, fight the fish in the water by reading its tells | Built, not yet seen in the Editor | [Fishing](../2-gameplay/skills/fishing.md) |
| **Death with a ferryman** | Everything you die with is held until you pay, in coin or an obol, or walk the long way back through the realm of the dead for free | Built | [death](../2-gameplay/death.md) |
| **A world in revision** | Places that appeared, moved or were never finished; old maps true only in the Dreaming; revised fish | Designed; first pieces built | [universe](../3-story/universe.md) |
| **A hand-made continent** | 37 areas, each with one shape you remember, land first, reasons to come at every stage | Wave 1 being built | [world](../3-story/world.md) |
