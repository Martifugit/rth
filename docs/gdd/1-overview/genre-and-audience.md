# Genre and audience

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/systems.md`.*

## Genre

An online RPG: a skill-based sandbox with real-time ability combat on a tile grid. Its modifiers:

- **Tick-based.** Everything advances in 0.6-second ticks: one tile a tick walking, two running.
- **Ability combat** in the manner of RuneScape 3's: builders, spenders and ultimates on a global
  cooldown, with an adrenaline resource (phlegm).
- **Long skilling loops:** gather, process, use, each skill to level 100.
- **A sandbox with no forced faction,** quests and bosses on top.
- **Persistent:** one world that is always there, saved as you play.

## Audience

- **Who:** an invited group of friends, at most about 200 players on one private server.
- **How they play together:** in the same world, sharing it rather than competing for it. They
  never fight each other. Resource nodes are shared (and busy spots refill faster), a forge's draw
  helps everyone at it, a reliquary on a monument boosts everyone empowering it. There is no
  market to speak of, so trading is between friends, not an economy.
- **What they want:** a RuneScape-like game they can play for a long time, which isn't RuneScape:
  its own world, its own story, its own systems.
- **What they don't need:** protection from each other (no anti-cheat, no PvP), and monetisation
  of any kind.
