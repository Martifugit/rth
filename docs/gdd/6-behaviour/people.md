# People

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `CLAUDE.md` (M5).*

Townsfolk can't be attacked. They stand at their work (a counter, a yard, a monument) and talk when
spoken to; what they say changes with your quests and what you have done. A conversation ends when you
walk away, except a few that follow you (Pennick's on the Landing).
