# Creatures

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `CLAUDE.md` (M2, D3).*

- **Wandering:** a creature moves about near where it lives.
- **Aggression:** some attack anyone who comes near; most only fight back.
- **Chasing:** a creature chases its target, by its own rules for melee or ranged reach and line of
  sight.
- **Giving up:** led too far from home, it gives up and goes back.
- **Patrols:** some walk a beat, back and forth or round a loop, and go back to it after a chase.
- **Death and return:** a killed creature leaves its carcass and comes back after a while.
- **Some can't be fought** (the shades of the long way back); some hit for a share of your blood, so
  they are as hard on everyone.
