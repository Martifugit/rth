What happened before the player woke, as far as it is known to the design. Most of it is not known
to anyone in the world, or is told differently there.

## Before history

The world was made unfinished, and has been revised every few ages since. The last revision ended
about a thousand years ago. What it added, moved and took out is remembered as catastrophe and
legend, and in scholars' lists.

## The Red Oath, and the empire's founding

Long ago, a river people swore on a flat stone below the hill where Cruor stands now, to a god made
of oaths sworn in blood: p-sworn. It keeps every oath exactly, whatever it costs and whoever pays. Its
keepers taught three rules: *never swear in anger, never swear for anyone but yourself, never swear
for ever.*

After raiders burned the river villages (said to be from the hill people of the high valleys; never
proved), three of the people who kept the rules best broke all three at once. **Brand**, the river's
war-leader, **Aud**, the keeper of the stone, and **Jennet**, the healer, swore the **Red Oath**: *we
and our children will not rest until not one of them is left alive under the sky.* Hundreds swore after
them. For eleven years the sworn did not sleep, and the war followed the hill people across the world,
destroying three other peoples for sheltering them.

In a cave at the end of it the three found a boy with Jennet's daughter's eyes, her grandson, half
hill blood. Unable to stop their own hands, they swore one last oath, *against* their god, which it had
to keep: to tear the Red Oath out of it. They did. The oath went into them, and their bodies broke and
fused into one abomination with three faceless heads and the Red Oath beating in its chest. p-sworn,
torn open, took their faces: its first collateral. The boy's face went out of the world's memory and he
walked away a stranger, the first of **the Faceless**. Everyone who had sworn slept for three days, and
woke to find the war over and their god wounded.

p-sworn has bled ever since. It now takes blood first, as collateral for every oath, and calls it in
when an oath breaks. The river people fed it what blood they could, built monuments, and the monuments
became an empire.

**What the church tells:** the war was **the Founding**, a holy war against a nameless, devilish enemy.
At the hour of victory three of its champions turned on their god out of envy and were cast out:
**the Traitor**, a three-headed devil, the root of every evil. It doesn't say who started the war, and
it certainly doesn't say the god is still bleeding.

## A torn god

Long ago a god was torn apart; each piece became a demigod with a life of its own, none knowing what it
was ([p-scattered](powers/scattered.md)). By whom and why is sealed.

## The moon

One of the two moons cracked. The first fragment tore the crater of Selenfall through the spine's crest.

## The night before the game

Tolling's bell rang for a death: fishers had pulled a stranger out of Wennow, and the church laid the
body in the dead-house under the bell tower. In the morning the stranger got up. That is the player.
