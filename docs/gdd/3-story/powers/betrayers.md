# p-betrayers

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore-rework/entities/untroth.md` (canon, 2026-10-24) and
`docs/lore.md` II. "The Untroth" is the proposed name; their followers are the Faceless.*

## Summary

Three of p-sworn's first followers, **Brand**, **Aud** and **Jennet**, fused into one body around the
oath they tore out of their god. Their faces were taken. One at a time they walk the world in the
borrowed faces of trusted people, and they will take anyone's face out of the world's memory, so no
oath can find them.

## Back story

See [back story](../back-story.md). Brand led the river's spearmen and spent his life talking feuds
down; he spoke the Red Oath first. Aud kept the stone and knew how an oath is shaped; she gave it its
words, and later found the way to end it. Jennet was the healer; she swore it, and found her own
grandson at the end of it.

## The body

The size of a barn, more arms than it needs and not all of them finished, three necks rising from one
pair of shoulders, three heads as smooth as eggs. In its chest the Red Oath still beats, still sworn
and unkept, and it still wants the hill people's blood, which by now is in half the world. **Two heads
must hold the heart down at all times,** so only one can go out.

## Borrowed faces

A head reaches into something lesser (a hob, the spirit of a well, the small grey things that gather
where people sleep) and wears it like a glove, shaped into a face it has seen. Brand wears soldiers,
Aud the people who write things down, Jennet the people who look after you. A lesser thing learns a
face the way still water does, so **the face is always mirrored**: the parting, the scar, the ring on
the other side. **Their sign:** your name, spoken behind you, in the voice of someone you trust; nobody
is there when you turn.

## What they want

They have never agreed. **Brand:** give the oath back, let p-sworn heal and the three die as people;
but the Red Oath would go back unkept, and loose on half the world. **Jennet:** never; hold it down for
ever. **Aud:** says nothing, but has started asking about places that aren't where the old maps put
them, and about the heap where the world throws away what it no longer wants.

## The Faceless

Give yourself to one of them and they take your face out of the world: your mother passes you in the
street, and no oath you swore can find you. Deserters, the conquered, people sworn at spear-point come
to them. To the empire they are the Traitor's people; to these, the only power that ever let anyone go.
The price is that nobody will ever know you again.

- The body as a late place and boss: three heads, one holding the heart, the other two fighting their
  own ways; if the fight takes all three heads' attention, the heart gets loose.
