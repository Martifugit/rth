## Summary

The god of Cruor's empire, **made of the oaths its followers swear in blood.** Swear in blood and its
strength is yours; break the oath and the blood is called in. Its sign is **a taste of iron in your
mouth**. It has never told a lie and never broken a promise, which is the worst thing anyone can say
about it: it keeps every oath exactly, whatever it costs and whoever pays, and it is always close at
the moments people are most likely to say something terrible.

## Back story

See [back story](../back-story.md). Its first followers betrayed it: three of them tore the Red Oath out
of it, and the wound never closed, because the torn-out piece still beats in another body. Since then
it takes blood first, as collateral for every oath.

## What it wants, and its weakness

- **Hunger:** oaths and blood. It starves without new oaths, so the empire must keep swearing in new
  peoples; it bleeds without new blood, so the empire must keep collecting. Both make it expand.
- **The secret:** the blood gathered at every monument is feeding the god and keeping it from
  bleeding out. The faithful are told it seals their oaths. Very few know.
- **Fear:** a world where nobody swears any more; and, secretly, bleeding out.
- War follows it, though it is not a god of war.

## Faces

To the empire, a keeper of faith. To the peoples it conquers, a debt collector.

## Relationships

Its church is the empire's (still the Sanguine Synod in the game). Its enemies are its betrayers (the
church's "Traitor") and their Faceless.

## In the game

- Religion: the church's hymns, melee's verses, monuments, pledges and scriptures
  ([Religion](../../2-gameplay/religion.md)).
- Unseen for now: felt in the taste of iron and the strength of sworn soldiers, spoken for by its
  priests. **It has a place in the world, and will make an entrance** (not in v1).
- Every boss of the church's martyrs (the Unquiet Saint).

## Sealed

What wounded it is decided (the Red Oath torn out of it); whether it existed before the first oath;
what would happen if the wound closed or consumed it; where its place in the world is.
