# The constants

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` II-III and `docs/lore-rework/gods.md`. Inspired by the
Endless of *The Sandman*, as a family of beings who are concepts, each with a realm.*

## Summary

Three siblings older than the gods. Each **is** part of how existence works; they are not worshipped
and want nothing from mortals. **The revisions never touch them**: a god can be taken, a constant
can't, and the gods are quietly afraid of them. They rarely meet; when they do, something in the
world shifts.

## The three

- **The dreamer** keeps **the Dreaming**, a landscape built from everyone's dreams where places stand
  as people remember them. The Dreaming skill gathers there, and the dreamer notices who takes what.
- **The keeper of the dead** keeps **Lowe**, the realm of the dead. **Pennick** the ferryman works for
  them.
- **The keeper of the spoil** receives everything the world rejects.

## In the game

Lowe's Landing and the long way back; the Dreaming, through the Dreaming skill and the nightmare
thread; the spoil, rarely and dangerously reachable from the deepest seams of Undermost (proposed).
