# p-scattered

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` II and `docs/lore-rework/gods.md`. Inspired by Osiris.*

## Summary

Long ago a god was torn apart. Its pieces didn't die: **each became a demigod**, with a life, a
character, followers and a place of its own. **None of them knows what they were.** Restoring the god
would cost each of them the life they have lived.

## In the game

- A quest line: the player helps them discover what they were, piece by piece. At its climax the
  player must **convince them to recombine** to face some threat. Convincing them is the hard part.
- The layout keeps five sites for demigods.
