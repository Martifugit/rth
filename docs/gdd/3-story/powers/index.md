# The powers

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` II and `docs/lore-rework/gods.md`.*

Gods and every kind of superhuman being exist and act in R'th. A god is a character, not a domain:
each is known by its story, its wound, its hunger, its bargain, its sign, its faces, its presence and
its fear. Their presence is uneven, and no single rivalry runs the world. Many grow from myths,
reimagined so the inspiration stays discoverable.
