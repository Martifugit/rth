## Summary

An enormous face in a cliff, carved by nobody remembers whom, with **a waterfall pouring from its
mouth**. Drink from the falls and you see what it remembers, and leave one memory of your own in the
water. Thousands of years of drinkers mean it holds memories from before the revisions: removed
places, moved places, forgotten peoples. **It is the only mind that does.**

## In the game

- It is carved in the Scarp, where the river from Gall's plateau pours from its mouth into a pool.
  Neutral ground: both sides of the war leave the face alone. A village, **p-brow**, lives on its
  brow.
- **One day, the falls will stop flowing.**
