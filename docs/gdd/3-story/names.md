# Names

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/world-map.md` (names kept and picked) and
`docs/lore-rework/names.md` (proposals).*

The owner names things. A place gets a proper name with a meaning under it: short, easy to say
aloud, something a player could shout across a chat channel. Placeholders are written `p-something`
and never reach players; until a name is chosen the game keeps its current wording.

## Picked

Cruor, Sere, Cistern, Meridian, Frostpeak Forge, Undermost, Selenfall, House Gall, Umber, Mordant,
Sallow, the Faceless, Tolling, Widdermill, Wennow, Palmerway, **Lowe** (the realm of the dead) and
**Pennick** (the ferryman), both picked 2026-10-23. In the betrayers' story: **Brand**, **Aud**,
**Jennet**.

## Working names (used in the layout and the area list, not yet confirmed)

Knellmere (the great lake), Eller (the forest), Kiln (Gall's city), Skerrin (where the lesser houses
meet), Adit (the dwarves' southern hold), Brack (the lesser houses' fens), Scalth (where the vitriol
mites and their salts come from).

## Still in the game until renamed

The Sanguine Synod (p-sworn's church), the Rationalists (the spine's surface people).

## Placeholders

p-sworn (Troth proposed), p-betrayers (the Untroth proposed), p-scattered, p-head, p-constants,
p-spoil, the walker, and the layout's p- places.

## Retired

Tithe, Calcinia and its places, the Cradle and the Quaere, the Verdigris Moon, the Assayer, the
Dreamweaver, the Physician, the Covenant, the Schism, the Scalding.
