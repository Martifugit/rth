# Fen rat

*Draft (2026-11-03). Built (Milestone 4).*

## Summary

The first creature: big rats of the reeds, at the river's mouth below Tolling and in the reed fens on
the lake road. They fight back only when attacked.

## Kinds

| Kind | How dangerous | Aggressive | Where | What it gives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fen rat | very low: a new player's first fight | no | reed margins | a carcass: butchered at a skinning rack with a skinning knife for meat (a haunch), hide, gall (Dreamless needs three), and now and then a glass flensing hook |

## Behaviour

Wanders the reeds; fights back when attacked.
