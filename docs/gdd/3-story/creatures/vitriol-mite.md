# Vitriol mite

*Draft (2026-11-03). Built (Milestone 4).*

## Summary

Mites that breed where vitriol has leaked: in Sour Hollow, round a leaking cask dumped off Palmerway.
Their home is Scalth, the scalding pans on Brack's coast. The first creatures that attack on sight.

Attacks anyone who comes close, then chases a little way. Counted as occult-made by the verse
Abjuration.
