# Creatures

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 5, `docs/creatures.md` (a brainstorm, nothing decided) and
the game's content.*

Creatures are designed with the area they live in. Two rules hold for all of them:

- **Every creature belongs to a Butchering family** (wildlife, Scalth-touched, occult-made, war-beasts,
  moonborn, the deep), which says where it lives and what its carcass gives, or is a boss or a power.
- **A creature grows from a myth reimagined,** so the inspiration stays discoverable but the creature
  is R'th's own. Its look comes from the world (the humours, the revision, the cracked moon, the deep,
  blood oaths). Creatures that belong to one game or publisher are out.

**Where creatures come from in R'th** (the sources `docs/creatures.md` proposes, all from the canon):
the revision (species that appear or turn up where they never lived), the spoil (the rejected:
unfinished creatures that only half work), the Dreaming (animals as someone remembered them, slightly
wrong; the nightmare), the dead (the restless, the oath-bound), blood oaths (war-beasts, debt
collectors), the occult arts (bile-made flesh), the cracked moon (moonborn), the deep, and old beasts.
