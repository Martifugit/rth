# The dead of the long way

*Draft (2026-11-03). Built (D3). From `docs/lore-rework/death.md`.*

## Summary

What a player meets walking the long way back through Lowe. None of them can be fought.

## Kinds

| Kind | How dangerous | Aggressive | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shade | high: a strike takes a big share of your blood, the same for everyone | yes, within a couple of tiles | drifting on set paths across the ash flats, the stone field, the last causeway | the dead who didn't make it; it follows a little way and strikes |
| Grasping hands | medium | unseen until they take you | thickest on the short way through the stone field | hold you a few seconds while the cold bites, and hurt |

## Behaviour

Shades patrol back and forth; they don't know you and don't want to. Hymns and Defence's abilities
help a little against them; the cold and the hands ignore wards.
