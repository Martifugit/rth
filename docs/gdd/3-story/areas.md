# Every area in brief

*Draft (2026-11-03). Each area's seed (Ryan): its goals, its gameplay, its link to the story, what
it needs and its difficulty, with Baldwin's description and connections. From `world/areas.json`,
`docs/world-plan.md`, `docs/lore.md` and, for wave 1, the area briefs in `docs/areas/` (agreed
2026-10-27). Where nothing is decided the seed says "not set"; the paper design, written when an area
is next to be built, fills it in. The beat chart ([II](../2-gameplay/beat-chart.md)) is checked
against these.*

The continent is **37 areas** of three to eight regions each, built in five waves, nearest the start
first, plus the frontiers held back. Stages: early (tiers 1-3), mid (4-6), late (7-10).

## Wave 2

### The great bridge and the dry valley
*The north road over the great river, the dry valley and its bridge over nothing.* A valley whose river
was moved: the revision in the ground. Goals, gameplay, difficulty: not set. Story link: the revision
(a moved river). Connections: Cruor, Tolling's north (Hollow lane), the foothills salient.

### The salt pass and the high valley
*The nearest crossing of the spine: the high valley's terraces, Sere's river's tarn, the toll.* Alpine
pasture, terraced farms, observatories; a toll gate at the top and the view west over the frontier.
Story link: the surface people (every crossing pays); the frontier. Goals, difficulty: not set.

### Sere's river: the salient and the sluice
*Where the green dries into Sere: the sluice fortress and Sere's northern wells.* p-riverhold keeps a
sluice that could close Sere's river: the empire's hand on Sere's water. Story link: empire and Sere.
Goals, difficulty: not set.

### The Downs
*Chalk hills south of the lake, the white edge and its hill figure.* Rolling chalk hills with a steep
north-facing edge and long views south to the Scarp. Story link: the hill figure. Goals, difficulty:
not set.

### Selenfall's breach
*The crater through the crest and its approach from the dry farms.* The scar the first moon fragment
tore through the spine: a shaft without a visible bottom, descended by lift through three levels (the
Works, the Glass, the Hollows) to the heart. The surface people keep the crossing and the lift; below,
fewer people and more moonborn things, stranger the deeper you go. It is also the middle way over the
spine, guarded.
- **Goals:** show the player the moon is cracked and still falling.
- **Story link:** the cracked moon.
- **Difficulty:** early to mid for the surface and the first level; each level a step harder; the heart late.
- **State:** its surface is built (stage 1, approved by the owner 2026-10-23).

### The east road
*The great river's valley east of Cruor, p-forestgate, the ford villages.* Goals, difficulty: not set.

## Wave 3

### The front below the Scarp
*The war: the camps, the Frontline ramp, p-head's falls and the brow.* Open war along the Scarp; the
border town at the ramp's head changes hands; the face in the cliff on neutral ground. Story link: the
war; p-head. Goals, difficulty: not set.

### Cistern and the gravel plain
*Sere's capital on its mesa, the lone mesa, the salt pan.* Cistern on the deepest wells; the salt pan a
place with no history. Story link: Sere and its water; the revision (the pan). Not set otherwise.

### Eller
*The great forest: its edge, the old wood's heart, the woodland mere.* Not set.

### The north farms and the moor's edge
*Farms between Eller and the moors, and the climb onto them.* Not set.

### The foothills salient
*The empire's forts climbing the foothills where the great river leaves the mountains.* Not set.

### Meridian
*The surface people's capital on its shoulder, the great river's tarn, the Meridian pass.* Whoever
holds it holds the crossing; dry clear air for the observatories. Not set otherwise.

### The Wolds
*High sheep country east of Cruor, the old crater lake, the unmade disc.* An unfinished place and an old
moonfall crater. Story link: the revision; the moon. Not set otherwise.

### Brack's edge and the causeway
*Where the empire's fields give way to the fens; Causeholm and Mordant's caves.* Not set.
