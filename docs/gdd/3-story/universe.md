# The universe

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` I-II and `docs/lore-rework/universe.md` (decided by the
owner, 2026-10-22).*

R'th is an unfinished world. It was made, and the making was never completed. **Who or what made
it is unknown**, and will stay unknown for a long time; whatever it is, it is not present in the
world in any form people recognise.

## Revisions

The making happens in **revisions**, ages apart. In a revision, things are **added** to the world,
**moved** within it and **taken out** of it: a species, a river, a village, sometimes a whole
people. Between revisions nothing large changes, for centuries. The last revision ended **about a
thousand years ago**; now the signs of a new one are appearing, and **the game begins at that
turn**. Later updates can belong to it: a place appears, a place turns out to have moved, something
comes back.

**The game never removes places.** Removal belongs to history; in play, a revision shows itself by
what appears and what moves.

## How it reads in the world

Every mystery has three layers: what people **believe**, what scholars and ruins let them
**suspect**, and what is **true**, which is often **sealed** (undecided until a quest needs it, with
only its constraints fixed).

- **Ordinary people** remember catastrophes, extinctions, a lost city, a people that "came from
  nowhere". They see the effects and never the cause; to them the world is simply the world.
- **Scholars** keep lists nobody can explain: places on old maps that aren't on the ground; places
  on the ground but not where the records put them; places with no history at all; ruins of a style
  nobody made; records that contradict each other in the same way at the same date.
- **Unfinished places** exist inside the world: never quite completed, unnaturally regular. Scholars
  investigate them; most people avoid them.

## The spoil

Everything taken out of the world goes somewhere: **p-spoil**, a heap of everything rejected, piled
up age after age like a spoil heap beside a mine. Unfinished creatures, removed places, lost peoples
and things that only half work lie in layers, the deepest the oldest. It can be reached, rarely and
dangerously, and what's in it sometimes leaks back. Digging down through it is digging back through
the revisions.

## The humours

Blood, phlegm, and yellow and black bile are how bodies work in R'th. They are nature, not
cosmology: they give combat its identity and the peoples practise arts built on them, but they are
not the secret of the universe.

## The powers above mortals

- **The constants:** three siblings older than the gods, each part of how existence works, untouched
  by the revisions ([the constants](powers/constants.md)).
- **The gods:** a handful everyone has heard of (about six to ten) and many lesser powers beneath
  them. A god is a character, not a domain; every god can be taken in a revision, and all of them
  know it ([powers](powers/index.md)).
- **The Dreaming:** a landscape built from everyone's dreams, where places stand as people remember
  them, so moved places still stand where they used to. Old maps are true there.

## Two moons

One of the two moons is cracked, and fragments still fall. Selenfall, on the spine, is the scar of
the first. A fall marks an area for a while.
