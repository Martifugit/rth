# Clerk Ambrose

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` VII, The Bell and his dialogue.*

## Summary

The church's clerk and banker in Tolling, a treasury clerk with his ledger chained to his wrist. The
ledger has the player down as dead.

## Personality

Dry and exact.
> "Name? ...Yes. You're in the ledger. Everyone is, eventually."

## In the game

The bank (the church's strongbox). The Bell, steps 2 and 6: what was found on the body went into the
strongbox; at the end, the entry is struck out.
