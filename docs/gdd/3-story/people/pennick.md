# Pennick

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` III, `docs/lore-rework/death.md` and his dialogue. Named
2026-10-23 (from *penny*).*

## Summary

The ferryman of Lowe, who works for the keeper of the dead. He takes the dead back across, and holds
everything they died with until they pay. He holds an oar he never seems to need.

## Personality

Businesslike, faintly amused.
> "You died. Everyone does once; your sort, more than once."

## In the game

Death ([II](../../2-gameplay/death.md)). The first time, he explains; he notices an obol and takes it.
**He already knows the player** the first time they die.

## Open questions

1. The owner's idea for the bell's quest line: he found it funny to have the player wake in the
   dead-house, because it made them come back to him with interesting stories.
