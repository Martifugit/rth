# Brother Hollis

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` VII, `docs/skills.md` 4.4 and his dialogue.*

## Summary

A cantor of Cruor who keeps the church's monument on Palmerway, east of Tolling. His forearms are a
ladder of neat white scars.

## Personality

Devout, plain, sure.
> "Blood keep you, pilgrim. Or are you the sleeper from Tolling? Either way. You've come to the right stone."

## In the game

Religion's first teacher: he explains the founts ("a little, often") and trades scriptures for pledges;
he sells the first melee weapons. He believes the blood seals the oath.
