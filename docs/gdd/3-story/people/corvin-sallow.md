# Corvin Sallow

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` VII, `docs/occultism.md`, Dreamless and his dialogue.*

## Summary

Tolling's friendly apothecary, who sells the player their first potions. Secretly an occultist: the
last son in the empire of the fallen house of Sallow. Far more curious about the player than an
apothecary should be.

## Back story

Sallow is a minor occult house fallen on hard times; its ruin stands on a drowned holm in Brack. Corvin
lives inside the empire that hunts his kind, selling remedies.

## Personality

Warm, quick, a listener who files everything away.
> "Corvin Sallow: apothecary, tooth-puller, listener. Sit, if you like. Sleepers who wake are rarer than you'd think."

## In the game

- **Shops:** Sallow's Apothecary (food, tonics, vials, a skinning knife); a back room for those he trusts.
- **Tasks:** vitriol mites and their glands, for his favour (from Butchering 10).
- **Quests:** The Bell (step 3: looks the player over); Dreamless (brews the draught, and wants to hear
  everything Edda says).
- **His secret:** a hidden still where the first occult rites are worked; he teaches the first, crude
  but relatively safe rites and the Sallow Primer (designed).

## Relationships

Edda (he is very interested in where she goes at night); the occult houses; the empire, which would
burn him.
