# The walker

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` III and the walker's dialogue. Not named (Tamsin Fare is
proposed).*

Someone who tried the long way back in life and failed, and has spent the afterlife on the far shore
helping those who make it. A traveller in a worn cloak, leaning on a staff: the only warm colour on
that shore.
> "I tried it once, when I still had a body to try with. I didn't make it. I've waited here since, for the ones who do."

Reach them and they give back everything Pennick holds, free.
