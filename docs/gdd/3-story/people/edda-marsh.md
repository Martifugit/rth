# Edda Marsh

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` VII, Dreamless and her dialogue.*

## Summary

A Tolling woman who sleepwalks by the pond and says things she shouldn't know. Her eyes are open; she
isn't.

## Personality

Talks in her sleep, in fragments that sound like nonsense and aren't.
> "...the tide is wrong. It comes in twice and only goes out once. Somebody should tell them."

## In the game

- **Dreamless:** she goes somewhere grey at night, full of threads and someone counting, and wants it
  to stop. The player fetches Corvin's dreamless draught; asleep, she says something she shouldn't
  know.
- **The Bell** (step 5): asleep on her feet, she says something only the ferryman would know.
- Her pond is kept for the first Dreaming orb (proposed).

## Relationships

Corvin; the Dreaming and the nightmare thread.
