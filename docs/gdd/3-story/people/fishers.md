# The fishers

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/quests/the-bell.md` and `docs/lore.md` VI. New; not named.*

The fishers who pulled the player out of Wennow. They keep their boats at the landing on Wennow's
south shore. In The Bell (step 4) they show where the player was found; the Tolling plan proposes
they teach fishing and cooking there.
