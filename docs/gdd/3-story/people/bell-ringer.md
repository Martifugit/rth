# The bell-ringer

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/quests/the-bell.md` and `docs/lore.md` VI. New; not named.*

## Summary

Tolling's bell-ringer, who rang the bell for the drowned stranger and finds them up and about in the
morning. He looks twice. The bell has never been wrong before.

## In the game

The Bell: at the foot of the dead-house stairs (step 1), sending the player to the clerk; at the end,
saying the bell was cast as one of a pair, the other hanging in a belfry now drowned in Knellmere.
His house is in Tolling; the ringing chamber is his.
