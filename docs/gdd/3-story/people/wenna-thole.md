# Wenna Thole

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` VII and her dialogue.*

## Summary

Master of Tolling's skinning yard: everything that crawls out of the fens ends up on her racks. Her
forearms say she means it.

## Personality

Blunt.
> "You're the one who woke. Half the town's whispering about you and the other half's lying about it."

## In the game

Butchering's first teacher and task-giver (wildlife): fen rats, then mites, for yard tokens, spent in
her yard's shop (hide gear, the glass flensing hook).
