# Threads

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` V and the sealed tables in `docs/lore.md` and

The long stories the world is built to carry. Each is open beyond what is written here. Every quest
line should belong to one or more of them.

| Thread | What it is | Where the player first touches it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The revision** | Places appearing, places that have moved, things leaking out of the spoil, quiet gods stirring. | The player themselves: the bell rang for them, and they came back. Revised fish. |
| **The bleeding god** | What the empire's blood is really for, and what wounded p-sworn. | Every monument; Brother Hollis on Palmerway. |
| **The Faceless** | Who among the people you trust is one of them, or one of their gods. | Not placed yet. The sign: your name, behind you, in a trusted voice. |
| **The demigods** | Helping p-scattered's pieces learn what they were, and one day asking them to give up their lives and become one again. | Not placed yet. |
| **The face in the cliff** | What it remembers, and what happens when the falls stop. | The falls on the Scarp. |
| **The nightmare** | Something loose from the Dreaming, moving from sleeper to sleeper. Those it passes through wake with pieces missing, or don't wake. | Edda Marsh's sleepwalking (Dreamless). |
| **The cracked moon** | Fragments still fall. A fall marks an area for a while: strange growth, moonborn creatures, and for the largest, a world boss. | Selenfall, seen from the western march. |
| **The deep** | Something is bound beneath the spine, below the dwarves and below Undermost. The dwarves keep the seals but did not make them. | Not placed yet. |
