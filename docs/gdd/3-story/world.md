**The spine** runs the whole height of the west, from the warm south north into the cold; everything
east of it drains east and south. Three great lines divide the eastern land: **the great river** (west
to east across the empire's north, to the sea), **the Scarp** (the escarpment that lifts Gall's
volcanic plateau above the empire) and **the edge of the rain shadow** (where the green dries into
Sere, just south of Tolling).

## How the land is read

- **Land first, then people:** a player should be able to guess why a town is where it is (a crossing,
  a spring, a harbour, a defensible rock, a pass).
- **Borders follow the land.** Crossing from one power to another is a moment: a bridge, a ramp up a
  cliff, a pass, a causeway, a gate.
- **One shape you remember per area,** seen from inside it: the lake, the meander round Cruor's hill,
  the Scarp, the crater, the tors, the sea stacks.
- **The world is read from its roads,** with a beat every 60 to 100 tiles on a main road (a
  settlement, a landmark, a junction, a bridge, a view). Each power builds its own roads.
- **Landmarks to steer by:** the spine always west, the sea east, the cold north up, and a skyline
  landmark in each area.
- **Danger reads before it bites.** The heartland round Cruor is green, settled and safe; each
  periphery has an easy fringe and a harsh core, emptier, darker and stranger on the way in.
- **Natural gates:** the waygate on Palmerway, the toll passes, the ramps up the Scarp, the sluice,
  the fen causeways, Selenfall, the long road north. A gate is a place, and later a quest or a
  requirement.
- **The lore is in the ground:** unfinished places (unnaturally regular), a dry valley whose river was
  moved, a road that ends at nothing, old moonfall craters, the frontiers where the world is still
  being made.
- **Real height, planned:** height is spent where it tells the story and shapes the routes (the
  spine's walls and passes, the Scarp, the volcanoes, Cruor's hill); towns stay level, and their
  height is built (towers, floors) or below ground.
- **Room to grow:** frontiers on three sides, and sites kept in every area for content not yet
  designed.

## Size

About 1.37 million tiles of core land: 4 cities, 13 towns, 34 villages and 22 hamlets, with 63 sites
kept. Tolling to Cruor is about 340 tiles by road, under four minutes' walk. Most of the land between
places is sparse: a road, a few trees, a landmark.
