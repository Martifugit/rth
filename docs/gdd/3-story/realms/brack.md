# Brack, the lesser houses' fens

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` IV and `docs/world-plan.md` 3. Brack is a working name.*

**Who holds it:** the lesser occult houses, in defensible pockets and hidden cells; the empire's walled
port, **the enclave**, at the great river's mouth.

**The land:** low, wet, flat and broken: a maze of meres, channels and reed beds, with dry **holms**
holding the houses' holds and causeways between them, ending at the sea in **the broken coast** of
cliffs, sea caves, stacks and skerries. **Skerrin**, where the houses meet, among the sea stacks;
**House Mordant's sea caves**; **Scalth**, the scalding pans on the coast where the vitriol mites breed;
**House Umber's hold** in the northern meres near the cold; **Sallow's fallen house**, a ruin on a
drowned holm. Roads are causeways and plank walks.

**Skyline:** the sea stacks; the enclave's walls across the flat.

**What it offers:** occultism's houses, rites and spellbooks; the occult-made creatures; the top herbs
and fibres of the deep fens.
