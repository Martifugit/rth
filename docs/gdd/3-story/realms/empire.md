# Cruor's empire

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` IV and `docs/world-plan.md` 3. Venaria, for the heartland,
is a name under review.*

**Who holds it:** Cruor's empire and p-sworn's church, about a third of the land, the largest army.

**The land:** the rich heart of the continent east of the spine: rivers, farmland round villages,
a great lake, a great forest and roads, with Cruor in the middle on a hill in the great river's loop.
Green, settled and the safest land in the world. Its roads out in the land are gravel paths and dirt
tracks that wander in easy bends; paving is for towns.

**What is here:**
- **Tolling and Wennow,** the start, a low river basin where the green begins to dry.
- **Palmerway and the lake road** east to **Knellmere**, a great lake with a drowned belfry.
- **Cruor** on its hill, the cathedral at its crown, seen from everywhere in the heartland.
- **Eller,** the forest north of the great river; **the Downs,** chalk hills with a figure cut in them;
  **the Wolds,** high sheep country down to the east coast.
- **The western march,** dry downs between Tolling and the spine's foothills.
- **The salients:** the foothills salient below Meridian, the riverhold on Sere's river, the fortress
  pocket on Gall's plateau, the enclave at the great river's mouth in the fens.
- Monuments of the church along the roads.

**Skyline:** Cruor's hill and spire; the hill figure; the spine always to the west.
