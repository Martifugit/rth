# Below: Undermost and the deep

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` IV-V and `docs/skills.md`.*

- **The dwarves' caves** inside the spine: nobody else can find their way.
- **Undermost,** the depths beneath the dwarves, held by older peoples nobody visits on purpose. The top
  ores (deepglass, bindiron) and the deep's Butchering family.
- **The deep:** something bound beneath the spine, below the dwarves and below Undermost. The dwarves
  keep the seals but did not make them, and keep Gall's fire away from them.

The layout keeps sites for the seals and a way into Undermost in the spine.
