# The frontiers

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` IV and `docs/world-plan.md`.*

The world is not closed. **West of the spine,** in **the far north** beyond the fjords, and out among
**the southern and eastern islands** lie frontiers: land where the world grows next, where frontier
peoples live, and where the revision may add more. They are drawn as land and seen from the passes,
the coasts and the ships, but not walked until a frontier opens.
