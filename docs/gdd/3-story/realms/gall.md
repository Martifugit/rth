# House Gall's plateau

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` IV and `docs/world-plan.md` 3.*

**Who holds it:** House Gall, the dominant occult house: openly occult, fire, loud, burns first.

**The land:** a raised volcanic table above **the Scarp**, the escarpment that is the front. Three
volcanoes: the great cone with **Kiln**, Gall's city, terraced on its northern flank, its fires seen
far down in the empire at night; an active cone near the east coast whose lava runs into the sea and
makes new land (the world growing, in front of you), with Gall's port beside it; and an old collapsed
one whose caldera lake feeds the river that falls from p-head's mouth. Between them grey ash plains,
lava fields crossed by black cinder roads, obsidian flats, hot springs, yellow sulphur flats.

**The front:** the Scarp's face is cliff, climbable only at a handful of ramps, and the ramps are the
war: Gall can't come down in force, the empire can't get up. A border town at the head of the great
ramp changes hands. The empire holds a **fortress pocket** on a sheer volcanic plug between the caldera
and Kiln: impossible to take, impossible to leave. Below the Scarp: camps, burned farms, a no-man's
strip.

**Skyline:** the cone and its plume.
