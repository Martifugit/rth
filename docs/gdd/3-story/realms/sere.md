# Sere

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` IV and `docs/world-plan.md` 3.*

**Who holds it:** Sere, a dry kingdom ruled by **Queen Saffiya**, from **Cistern**.

**The land:** the spine takes the rain; Sere gets what runs off it. **Sere's river** rises in the high
valleys west of Tolling and runs south through all of Sere to the sea, a green ribbon of farms in the
desert. The empire holds its upper course and its gorge, where **the riverhold** keeps a sluice that
could close the river; Sere pays. **Cistern** stands on a mesa in the gravel plain, away from the river,
on wells deeper than anyone else can dig: whatever the empire does, Cistern drinks. The badlands under
the spine (red mesas, canyons, the dwarves' southern hold **Adit**), the salt pan (a dry white lake bed
with mirages, a place with no history), the erg (dunes, an oasis town), the delta and Sere's port,
whose ferry goes south. Roads are caravan tracks from well to well, marked by cairns.

**Skyline:** Cistern's mesa; the spine's red southern teeth.

**What it offers:** saltfigs; the caravans; armies that march in die of thirst.
