# The cold north

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/world-plan.md` 3. Nivil is a name under review. Unclaimed.*

North of Eller the land climbs onto **the moors** (high, open, lumpy ground with granite tors, bogs and
standing stones), then a belt of **pines**, then **tundra** and snowfields down to a north coast of
**fjords**. Glacier tongues come down from the frozen spine. Trappers' camps, a frozen lake, frontier
peoples. Its roads are trodden paths and nothing else.

**Skyline:** the tors; the white wall of the frozen spine.
