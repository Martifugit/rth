# Lowe, the realm of the dead

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` III and `docs/lore-rework/death.md`.*

Kept by the keeper of the dead, one of the constants. What the living know of it is small:
**the Landing**, a small, very dark shore of ash in still black water, with a jetty, Pennick's boat,
lanterns, a bench and a grave chest; and, through the leaning stones on its south shore, **the long
way**, a causeway through dark water in four stretches, where the shades drift and hands wait in the
stone field. Beyond both, an endless dark lake.

Lowe becomes a real place over time; for now it is the death mechanic's ([death](../../2-gameplay/death.md))
and the long way back's ([activity](../../4-content/activities/the-long-way-back.md)).
