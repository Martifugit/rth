# The spine

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` IV and `docs/world-plan.md` 3-4.*

**Who holds it:** the surface people (still the Rationalists in the game) above, from **Meridian**;
the dwarves inside, trading from **Frostpeak Forge**; under a treaty.

**The land:** a range the height of the continent, broad and gentle on the east with spurs and
valleys, a **sheer face on the west** falling to the frontier. Along its length: the red teeth in the
dry south beside Adit; the high valleys west of Tolling (alpine pasture, terraced farms, observatories,
Sere's river's source, **the salt pass**, the nearest crossing); **Selenfall's breach**, the crater the
first moon fragment tore through the crest; **Meridian** on a terraced shoulder under the highest
peaks, above the great river's source, with the Meridian pass behind it; the frozen spine, glaciers to
the north coast, the frost pass and **Frostpeak Forge**, a hall cut into the last peak above the
northern sea. Its roads are switchbacks with **a toll at every crossing**. From every pass the frontier
is in view to the west.

**Skyline:** the whole range; Selenfall's notch; Meridian's domes.

**What it offers:** the ranged style and Artifice; observatories (they forecast moonfalls); the
dwarves' tools and metal; the great forge at Frostpeak Forge for special equipment.
