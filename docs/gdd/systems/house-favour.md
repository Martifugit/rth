# House favour

## Intent

House favour gives players reasons to travel to new [occult houses](../groups/occult-houses.md)
and their sites.

## Rules

1. A house's higher spells and rites ask for its favour as well as a level.

## Interactions

- [Spellbooks](../groups/occult-spellbooks.md): higher spells need favour.
- [Distillation](../activities/distillation.md): higher rites need favour.
