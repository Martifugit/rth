# Workflow

How pages move from a stub to agreed, when to edit and when to comment, how decisions are kept, and
how pages are named. The rules for what goes on a page are in [The Charter](charter.md).

## Status

Every page is a stub, a draft, agreed or cut. Anyone may move a page on, including to agreed.

## Edit or comment

- **Edit directly** on stubs and drafts: typos, links, structure, and detail that doesn't change the
  intent.
- **Comment first** when changing an agreed page, or when changing what someone else meant.

Short version: if it changes meaning on an agreed page, comment first.

## Decisions

- Every decision is one line in the [Decisions log](decisions-log.md): the date, the decision, and a
  link to the page it affects.
- The decision is also written into the page it affects, so the page stays the truth.
- Only big, contested choices get their own decision page, with the full why.

## Naming

- **Titles** are singular, named the way a player would say them, in sentence case: Goblin, Oak tree.
- **Groups** are plural: Goblins.
- **File names** are the title in lowercase with dashes (`oak-tree.md`), and unique across the whole
  GDD.
- **Placeholders** start with `p-` (p-sworn) until a real name is picked. Each one is a to-do.
