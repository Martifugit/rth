# Beat chart

*Draft (2026-11-03). Scott Rogers' beat chart: the player's journey through the world, beat by
beat, and what each beat gives them. It is filled in as far as areas are designed: wave 1 has
briefs; every other area has only its seed ([III, areas](../3-story/areas.md)). The area seeds must
fit this chart.*

## The first hours (wave 1)

| # | Area | Levels | Story beat | Skills it opens | Mechanics taught | Sends the player to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Tolling and Wennow](../4-content/areas/tolling/index.md) | new to about 15 | Waking in the dead-house; The Bell; Dreamless | Religion (the monument), Butchering, Fishing, Cooking, Medicine, Foraging, Woodcutting; tier 1 | talking, the bank, shops and potions, walking out, the first fight, the journal | east along Palmerway; west to the march; south to the landing and the dry rise |

stage 3.
