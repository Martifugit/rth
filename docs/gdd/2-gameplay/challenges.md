# Challenge structure

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `CLAUDE.md` (M5, M6, M8), `docs/skills.md` 5 and the content files.*

What a player can set out to do, besides training. Every kind below is built as a general mechanism,
so new ones are content.

| Kind | What it is | How it is tracked | Built |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Quests** | A story told in stages: talk, fetch, kill, make, use something on something. Each stage has its journal entry. Some need levels. Rewards are experience and items, and the story going on. | The journal: red not started, yellow in progress, green done; hover for the entry | yes |
| **Tasks** | A task-giver hands out a job (butcher so many carcasses, kill so many mites) for points in their own pool, spent at their shop. Every so many tasks in a row pays a streak bonus; cancelling costs points. | The task panel | yes: Wenna Thole, Corvin Sallow |
| **Achievements** | Things done round an area, in tiers from easy to master: climbing a tower, a record fish, a quest line finished. They send late players back across early areas. | The journal's achievement diary | 14, round Tolling |
| **Activities** | Bosses, trials and world events, repeatable. | Per activity | the long way back |
| **Collections** | The bestiary (kills, drops and carcass yields per creature); the fishing log (catches by weight); Dreaming's vignettes (designed). | The journal | the bestiary and the fishing log |
