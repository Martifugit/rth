# Death

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/lore.md` III, `docs/lore-rework/death.md` and `CLAUDE.md` (D1-D3).
Names picked 2026-10-23: the realm of the dead is **Lowe**, the ferryman **Pennick**.*

## Summary

When you die, everything you carried and wore goes to Pennick the ferryman, and you wake on a very
dark landing in Lowe, the realm of the dead. He takes you back to the living. Pay his fee and your
things come with you; carry an obol when you die and he takes that instead; or refuse him and walk
the long way back through the realm of the dead, and get it all back for nothing.

## Goals

- Make death cost something real without being cruel: there is always a free way back
  ([pillar 1](../1-overview/pillars.md)).
- Make death a place in the world, and the first glimpse of what lies under it ([pillar 2](../1-overview/pillars.md)).

## Ties to the setting

The dead go to Lowe, kept by one of the constants; Pennick works for them. Why the dead stay dead
and players come back is not explained. Pennick, meeting a player the first time they die, already
knows them.

## How it works

- **Nothing is kept.** Everything you die with goes into Pennick's hold.
- **His fee** is a share of what your things are worth, paid in coin from your pack, then your bank.
  Cheap gear goes free.
- **An obol** carried when you die is taken as the fee, whatever your gear is worth. It costs a pack
  slot and isn't cheap, but it is far cheaper than the fee on fine gear. It is never a menu choice:
  he says he noticed it.
- **No coin, no gear.** He takes you back without it and keeps it about a day; pay within that time
  and it is yours. Dying again adds to what he holds. There is no owing him.
- **The long way back:** a short, brutal walk through Lowe that is as hard for the strongest as for
  the weakest. At its end the walker gives everything back free.
  See [the long way back](../4-content/activities/the-long-way-back.md).
- **The Landing** has a grave chest (a bank), Pennick at the end of his jetty, and the leaning stones
  where the long way begins.

## What the player sees

A remark from Pennick in chat as you wake, then his conversation. The first time, he explains.

## Odd cases

- An obol withdrawn from the bank on the Landing does nothing: it only counts if you died with it.
- Paying returns you to the living with your things, at your spawn.
