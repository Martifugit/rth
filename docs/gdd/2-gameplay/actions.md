# Actions

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `CLAUDE.md` (M3, M5, E3).*

## Summary

Everything in the world is used the same way: walk up to it and act. Left-click does the obvious
thing; right-click lists everything you can do. Walking away cancels what you were doing.

## Goals

- One way of doing things everywhere, so new content needs no new controls.
- Make using one thing on another a way to discover things ([pillar 2](../1-overview/pillars.md)).

## How it works

- **People:** talk (a conversation of messages and numbered replies), trade, bank; townsfolk can't
  be attacked.
- **Objects:** open, gather, pray, climb, read, sit at a station to make things. An action can be
  locked until a quest or a level, with a line saying why.
- **Items:** wield or remove, eat or drink, drop, examine, and items that are used by themselves
  (clean a herb, rig a rod, open a fish box).
- **Using one thing on another:** pick up an item to use, then click an object, a person or another
  item. A carcass on a skinning rack butchers it; logs on a rock face lay a fire; an item shown to a
  person can start a line of conversation. Anything with no use says "Nothing interesting happens."
- **Picking up:** walk over a dropped item and take it. Loot is the killer's for a short while, then
  anyone's, then gone.
- **Busy:** whatever you are working on glows softly so you can see it.
