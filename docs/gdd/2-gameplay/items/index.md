# Items

*Draft (2026-11-03). Ryan's "game play elements": each kind of item, what it is for, how it is
rated. Exact numbers live in the game's data. From `docs/skills.md` and the content files.*

Items fill a 28-slot pack, ten worn slots and a bank. Most kinds follow the [tier
ladder](../progression.md): a tier-N item asks for level 10 x (N - 1), or 1 at tier 1.

| Kind | What it is for | How it is rated | Made by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Weapons** | Decide your style and auto-attack. Any kind fits (swords, maces, spears, scythes, crossbows, slings...); a kind's stats come from its grip (one-handed, off-hand, two-handed), so a kind is a choice of look, not of balance. | by tier and grip | Smithing (melee), Artifice (ranged) |
| **Armour** | Defence and a bonus to its own style: plate (melee), leathers (ranged), robes (occultism). Another style's armour costs that style accuracy. | by tier and slot | Smithing, Tailoring |
| **Jewellery** | A ring or amulet set with a gem; the gem's humour decides the style it leans to (garnet and bloodstone melee, amber the yellow pole, jet the black, moonstone ranged). | by the metal's tier | Smithing |
| **Tools** | Pickaxe, hatchet, sickle, rod, knives. The best one you can use is picked for you; two tools of a tier can differ (keener, more careful, one that chars logs to charcoal as it cuts). | by tier, plus an effect | Smithing, Artifice; Butchering's from task-givers |
| **Food** | Restores blood: about one hit's worth at its tier. Eating delays your next attack. | by tier | Cooking |
| **Potions** | Four doses: boosts, phlegm to start a fight, bile held at a pole, cures, devotion. | by level | Medicine |
| **Reagents** | Burnt by occult spells. | by tier and house | Occultism's rites (designed) |
| **Scriptures** | Burnt by verses. | four tiers | bought with pledges |
| **Carcasses** | Dropped by creatures, butchered for everything else. Unstackable, but can be noted. | by family | |
| **Remains** | Sealed in reliquaries for Religion. | by the creature | Butchering |
| **Trophies** | A fish landed whole, with its weight. Unstackable; sold at fish markets for fish boxes. | by size class | Fishing |
| **Obol** | Paid to Pennick if you die carrying it. | | sold (where: open) |
| **Capes** | Ordinary capes by Tailoring; a skill's cape at level 100. | | |

**Gear has no special place in the ladder at the top:** the best gear is special equipment, designed
piece by piece (usually a boss drop and a demanding process).

**A new character** starts with a little bread and two saltfigs, a tonic, a skinning knife, a
watchman's sword, a copper pickaxe and hatchet, a waterskin, a tinderbox, a fishing rod and a smith's
hammer, and wears an apprentice's athame and an occultist's robe. (The starter kit is the prototype's
and will change with The Bell, which hands back "what was found on the body".)
