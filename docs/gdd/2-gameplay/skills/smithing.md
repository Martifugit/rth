# Smithing

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 8, Smithing (decided 2026-10-21, the draw 2026-10-28).*

## Summary

Smelt ore into bars at a furnace (with charcoal from steel up), then smith bars into melee weapons,
plate, tools and metal parts at an anvil. The active layer is **the forge's draw**: everyone working
at a forge smiths faster while its fire roars.

## Goals

- The craft of melee gear, and the supplier of every skill's metal tools ([pillar 1](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).
- A busy smithy that is better for everyone in it.

## How it is trained

Make-X at the anvil. A forge's fire dies down slowly; working its bellows or feeding it charcoal now
and then keeps it roaring. Better charcoal holds it longer. A big piece takes more bars and gives more
experience. A sword is a sword wherever it is made.

## The ladder

Bars and gear of every tier, each needing Smithing at its tier's level. Every kind of melee weapon
fits (daggers to scythes and greatswords); plate is helm, body, legs, gauntlets, boots and shield.
Jewellery is a metal and a gem.

## Ties to other skills

Mining (ore), Woodcutting (charcoal), Melee and Defence (gear), every gathering skill (tools), Artifice
(heads and fittings), Religion (reliquary casings), Fishing (hooks).
