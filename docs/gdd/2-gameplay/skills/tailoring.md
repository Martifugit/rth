# Tailoring

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 8, Tailoring (decided in outline 2026-10-21). Not built.*

## Summary

The craft of soft armour: leathers for ranged and robes for occultists, plus capes. Tan hides and
weave fibre at a station, then sew.

## Goals

- Ranged's and occultism's armour ([pillar 1](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).

## How it is trained

Leather from hides (a hide's tier follows its creature); cloth from fibre and pale wool. Higher robes
are steeped in reagents.

## Ties to other skills

Butchering, Foraging, Occultism, Fishing (line and lures).
