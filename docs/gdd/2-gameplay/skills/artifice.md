# Artifice

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 8, Artifice (decided 2026-10-21). Not built.*

## Summary

The craft of the spine's surface people: ranged weapons of every kind and their ammunition at a
workbench, and fishing gear.

## Goals

- Ranged's supply ([pillar 1](../../1-overview/pillars.md)), and the fine tools other skills need.

## How it is trained

Weapons from parts (wood, metal fittings, a string of sinew or fibre); ammunition by tier from a head
(metal or stone), a shaft and fletching, made in sets; tipped ammunition (designed in outline); rods,
nets, traps and lanterns.

## Ties to other skills

Woodcutting, Smithing, Butchering, Mining (gems for tips), Ranged, Fishing.
