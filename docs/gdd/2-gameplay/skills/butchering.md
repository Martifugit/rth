# Butchering

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 5 (decided 2026-10-21) and `CLAUDE.md` M4.*

## Summary

The hub. Creatures drop **carcasses**, not loot; butchering a carcass at the right station with the
right tool gives meat, hide, sinew, feathers, glands, gall, tallow, remains and now and then a rare
item. Trained by butchering and by tasks.

## Goals

- Make killing things the way most materials enter the world, so combat and skilling meet at the
  carcass ([pillar 1](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).
- Tie every creature to the land it lives in ([pillar 3](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).

## Ties to the setting

Tolling's own trade is butchering: a road town on the edge of rough country takes in carcasses.
Each family's task-giver is someone of the land the family lives in.

## How it is trained

Bring the carcass to its family's station with its tool and butcher it. Carcasses don't stack, but
can be noted, and any station takes notes, so a long session needs one bank trip. Tasks from each
family's giver pay points for its shop: the family's tool, gear and boosts to yields.

## Ties to other skills

Meat to Cooking; hide and sinew to Tailoring and Artifice; feathers to Artifice; pale wool (mountain
goats) to Tailoring; gall, glands, tallow and bile-salt to Occultism and Medicine; remains to
Religion's reliquaries.
