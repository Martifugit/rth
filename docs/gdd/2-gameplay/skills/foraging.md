# Foraging

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 7, Foraging (decided 2026-10-21).*

## Summary

Herbs for Medicine, fibres for Tailoring, food for Cooking: the most widespread resource in the
game, filling the roadsides and the sparse land between places. Patches give several picks and
regrow; a sickle only speeds picking up. Herbs come out grimy and are cleaned with Medicine.

## Goals

- Always something to gather near the road ([pillar 3](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).
- A top herb that means roaming, not standing at one patch.

## The ladder

| Tier | Herb | Common in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | yarrow | green country, dry country; sparse almost everywhere |
| 2 | rue | lowlands |
| 3 | feverfew | green country, the fens' edges |
| 4 | bog myrtle | fens, mere shores |
| 5 | sere sage | dry country, foothills |
| 6 | saltwort | salt shores |
| 7 | frostcap (a fungus) | the cold heights |
| 8 | moonmoss | moonfall scars |
| 9 | hellebore | deep fens: single plants scattered through dangerous ground, each picked once and regrowing nearby |
| 10 | mandrake | the deepest fens, nowhere else |

**Fibres:** reed, flax, silkweed, threadmoss, gossamer (two homes each); pale wool comes from
Butchering. **Food:** berries, mushrooms, saltfigs (Sere), fen honey (with a smoker).

## Ties to other skills

Medicine, Tailoring, Cooking; seeds kept for Farming if it ever comes.
