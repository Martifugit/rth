# Skills

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 1 and 2 (the roster and the combat split decided
2026-10-21; the rules decided 2026-10-21 to 2026-11-03). Resource and tier names are provisional.*

Sixteen skills, each trained from 1 to 100 up the same ten tiers. RuneScape 3 has 29; R'th keeps the
list short because each skill is a pipeline one player runs for themselves, and every one feeds
something that matters in a fight ([pillar 1](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).

Each resource belongs to a kind of land and is thin elsewhere; **each skill's best tiers are in a
different land from the others'**, so no land is "the endgame". Where each tier sits on the new map
is decided area by area (the old regions it was drawn on are retired).

## Rules every gathering skill follows

- **Nodes** (rocks, trees, spots, patches, founts, orbs) are shared by everyone. Each attempt every
  few ticks succeeds by your level against the node's, plus your tool. A success may use the node up;
  it comes back after a while, sooner the more people work near it.
- **Every gathering and making skill can be done AFK at a base rate, and pays more for attention.**
  The active part is each skill's own, relaxed, and comes from **working with the place**: something
  about the node or station that a periodic, unhurried act keeps right. Not stamina, not spots to
  click, not rhythm.
- **Quirks** are kept to three or four tiers per skill (eel traps, a big shared elm, ore that only
  appears after a moonfall), so most of a ladder is plain and the quirks stay special.
- **Rare extras** (gems from ore, nests from trees, shells from fish) are the lucky rolls; the
  resource itself never is.

## Rules every making skill follows

- **Make-X:** choose what and how many at a station, and it runs until done or interrupted; a batch
  of 28 takes about a minute.
- **Stations** don't change by tier. Special equipment may need a special place; ordinary tiers never.
- **Failure** only where it reads naturally: food burns (less as you level). Everything else
  succeeds.
- **Better outputs** (designed, not built): a recipe can give more or different things at a
  particular station, with an unlock or a tool.
