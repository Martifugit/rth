# Melee

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 3.3 and 4.2. Built in Milestone 10.*

## Summary

Attack and strength in one skill: swords, maces, spears, anything held. The close, hard-hitting
style that wants to be hit, because its power comes from blood lost. Trained by melee damage.

## Goals

- A style whose engine is the church's verses, as bile is occultism's ([pillar 4](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).
- The low-blood way of fighting: power for staying alive on the edge.

## Ties to the setting

Melee is Cruor's empire's style: its blood-knights swear in blood to p-sworn and read verses that
cost them their own blood. Brother Hollis sells the first weapons on Palmerway.

## How it is trained

Melee damage. Abilities: builders (a heavy cut, a bleeding cut, a blow stronger the lower your blood,
a lash that costs your blood for phlegm), spenders and ultimates (big hits stronger at low blood, a
deep bleed, a vow that raises your damage). Names are provisional. Its power engine is
[Religion's verses](../religion.md).

## The ladder

Weapons of every tier (Smithing), each needing Melee at its tier's level. Spenders at 5, ultimates at
10. Abjuration (a verse) also asks for Melee 15.

## Ties to other skills

Smithing makes its weapons and plate; Religion powers it; Butchering's remains feed its monuments.
