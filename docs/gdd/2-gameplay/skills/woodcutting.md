# Woodcutting

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 7, Woodcutting (decided 2026-10-21, tending 2026-10-28).*

## Summary

Logs, charcoal and resin, with a hatchet. Trees are cut back to a stump and regrow; they don't fall.
The active layer is **tending**: the heartland's trees are coppiced and pollarded, and caring for
them makes them give more.

## Goals

- An active layer that is care, not clicking ([pillar 5](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).
- Fair to everyone at a stand: a tended tree helps whoever chops it.

## How it is trained

Chop a tree; each attempt succeeds by level. While someone chops in a stand, now and then one of its
trees needs its stool tending (suckers and brambles round its foot, seen only by those chopping
there). Tend it, and for a while it gives logs more often and is seldom cut back, for everyone; the
chopper sees "Well tended".

**Charcoal:** burn logs at a kiln; better logs give more. Steel and the higher smelts need it.
