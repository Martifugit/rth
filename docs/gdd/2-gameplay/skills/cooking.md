# Cooking

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 8, Cooking.*

## Summary

Fish and meat into food on a range (in a town) or a campfire (anywhere, which burns more). Food
heals about one hit's worth at its tier. Burning stops fifteen levels above a food's tier.

## Goals

- Every fight's food from your own catch or kill ([pillar 1](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).

## How it is trained

Make-X at a range or a fire lit from your own logs. Pies and stews from tier 3 (designed).

## Ties to other skills

Fishing, Butchering (meat), Foraging (fruit, herbs), Woodcutting (fires).
