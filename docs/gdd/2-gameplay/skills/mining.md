# Mining

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 7, Mining (decided 2026-10-21, fire-setting 2026-10-28).*

## Summary

Ore, gems, salt and stone, with a pickaxe. Mining works by **progress**: each strike fills a bar
towards the next ore, faster with level and a better pick, and the active layer is
**fire-setting**, the old miner's way of cracking hard rock.

## Goals

- Always worth mining the highest ore you can: higher ores fill slower and pay more, so there is no
  power-mining low tiers ([pillar 5](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).
- An active layer that works with the rock, not against a timer.

## How it is trained

1. Strike the rock; a bar over it fills; when it fills, the ore comes off and the rock may be worked
   out for a while.
2. **Set a fire:** use logs on the rock. The fire is the rock's, for anyone mining it, and burns a
   few minutes.
3. **The stone heats** in a cycle; its cracks glow brighter round the fire.
4. **Douse it** with water you carry, at the glow's peak for a full crack, early or late for less.
   Your crack is yours alone.
5. **A cracked face** multiplies your progress until you have worked the crack out; then it can heat
   again. About one douse a minute keeps it going.

## The ladder

| Tier | Ore | Where it tends to be | Quirk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | copper | river cliffs, road cuttings | the plain one |
| 2 | iron | hills, quarries | the workhorse |
| 3 | hematite | old church quarries, red hills | "the rock bleeds"; smelted with charcoal into steel |
| 4 | bog iron | fens, marsh pits | dredged from wet pits that refill |
| 5 | cinnabar | dry red country | |
| 6 | glare-silver | salt flats, white stone | |
| 7 | frostiron | the spine's heights, dwarven halls | |
| 8 | verdigris | moonfall craters | appears only after a moonfall, then fades |
| 9 | deepglass | Undermost | brittle: can shatter before it is worked out |
| 10 | bindiron | Undermost's deepest workings | few rocks, slow |

**Gems** come rarely from any rock, each carrying a humour: garnet (blood), amber (yellow bile), jet
(black bile), moonstone (phlegm, mid tiers up), bloodstone (pure blood, rare, high tiers).
**Salt:** rock salt (low), sere salt (mid), glare salt (high). **Stone** never runs out: sling
ammunition and shortcuts.

## Ties to other skills

Smithing (ore), Woodcutting (logs for fires), Occultism (salts), Medicine and Cooking (salt), Ranged
(stone, gem tips), jewellery (gems).
