## Summary

The arts of the occult houses: pushing your bile hot or cold for power. Three layers: abilities
that move the bile axis and cost only phlegm; a house **spellbook** whose chosen spell your
auto-attacks cast, burning **reagents**; and the **rites** that make those reagents. Trained by
occult damage and by rites.

## Goals

- The style of the bile axis: the riskiest, most unusual system, and the first one built ([pillar 4](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).
- A reason to travel: each house's spellbook and rites are at its own sites ([pillar 3](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).
- Self-made ammunition: the rites ([pillar 1](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).

## Ties to the setting

The occultists practise arts of bile: transformation, fire, solvents, flesh that won't settle. Their
arts make them terribly strong and slowly strange. Each house has its own philosophy of the flesh,
explosive yellow or corrosive black, and its own book. Corvin Sallow, Tolling's apothecary, is
secretly one of them, and teaches the first, crude but safe rites.

## How it is trained

- **Abilities:** a builder that pushes yellow, one that pushes black and heals, one that pulls bile
  home; spenders and ultimates at each pole that need a lean of a third or more (Gall Spark, Black
  Draught, Still the Humours, Bitter Salt, Choleric Flare, Melancholy Shroud, Vitriol Rain, Black
  Sun).
- **Spellbooks** (designed): one per house, learned from that house, swapped freely. Your active
  spell is your auto-attack, and each cast burns one to three reagents.

| Spellbook | House | Spells from level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Sallow Primer | Sallow (Corvin) | 1 |
| The Book of Gall | Gall | 15 |
| The Umber Codex | Umber | 15 |
| The Mordant Formulary | Mordant | 25 |

- **Rites** (designed): take a primary (a humour extract from Butchering), a base (salt, ash or brine)
  and from tier 7 a catalyst (a Dreaming fragment or a rare drop) to a house's site, set them in the
  vessel and let the rite run. Every so often a **disturbance** shows (a boil-over, a crack in the
  glass, a black bloom); answer it in time and the batch stays whole. Each site makes one family of
  reagents, as an altar makes one kind of rune. The first site is Corvin's still in Tolling.
- **House favour** from each house's rites and quests opens its higher sites and spells.
