# Defence

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 3.2 and 3.6.*

## Summary

Not a style but what every style stands on: the level armour asks for, your max blood, and four
defensive abilities any weapon can use. Trained by a share of combat experience (the Train defence
setting) and, designed, by damage your defensive abilities prevent.

## Goals

- One survival skill for all three styles, so armour of every family asks for the same level.

## How it is trained

Combat experience split by Train defence (half by default). Its abilities, on their own tab: Brace
(block part of the next hit, level 1), Steel the Flesh (20), Riposte (reflect part of a melee hit,
40), Unyielding (survive one lethal hit, long cooldown, 70).

## The ladder

Max blood grows with every level (about ten times as much at 100 as at 1). Armour of each tier needs
Defence at its level.
