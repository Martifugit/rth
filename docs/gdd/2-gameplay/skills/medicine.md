# Medicine

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 6.*

## Summary

The herblore: potions for boosts, phlegm, bile, cures and devotion, made at an apothecary bench from
a herb, a vial of water and a secondary from another skill. Not for healing, apart from one mild
draught; food heals. Potions have four doses.

## Goals

- The fight's edge: boosts and the bile draughts ([pillar 4](../../1-overview/pillars.md)), made by the
  player ([pillar 1](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).

## Ties to the setting

Humoral medicine: purging and balancing. Hellebore, the real plant used to purge black bile, is near
the top. Corvin Sallow introduces it in Tolling.
