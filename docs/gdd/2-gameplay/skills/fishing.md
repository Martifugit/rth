## Summary

Fish for Cooking, brine for rites, and extras for Medicine. Three ways to fish, each paying more for
more attention: **spots** you cast at and wait; **reading the water** to be where a spot comes back;
and **trophy angling**, where you find a species' trophy spot, rig your own tackle and fight the fish
in the water.

## Goals

- Something to do with fishing at every level, and a reason to come back to old waters
  ([pillar 3](../../1-overview/pillars.md)): records, and revised fish.
- An engaged way to fish that is still relaxed ([pillar 5](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).

## Ties to the setting

Fish are phlegmatic, so fish extracts are Medicine's base for phlegm. Rare **revised** fish are being
remade by the revision: a second fin, eyes on the wrong side. The collector, a scholar of the
revision, wants them.

## How it is trained

- **Spots** move instead of running out. Each comes and goes on its own timer.
- **Reading the water:** a spot may bubble shortly before it comes back. Cast into the bubbles and be
  there when the fish arrive, and you gain a stack of **Fish whisperer**, a better catch chance that
  wears off quickly. AFK, you fish a spot until it goes.
- **Trophy angling:** each species has five trophy spots, two active at a time, each shown by the
  species' own ripple. Rig rod, reel, line, hook and lure for the species, cast, and fight the fish
  where it is: it shows as a whirlpool, the line is the tension, its tells are water effects (a swirl
  before a turn, bubbles before a dive, a wake before a run). Pull against its turns, let go when it
  runs or leaps; tire it, and it comes in. Each tier teaches one new move. Catches go into the
  fishing log by weight; trophies go to fish markets for fish boxes; revised fish go to the collector
  for gear that removes a trade-off. A spot is spooked for you after a few catches.

## The ladder

| Tier | Fish | Home | Quirk; new in a trophy fight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | perch | rivers | turns |
| 2 | eel | rivers, meres | traps (set, leave, collect); dives |
| 3 | pike | lakes and slow rivers | runs; needs a wire leader |
| 4 | lamprey | fens and river mouths | latches to a stone |
| 5 | cistern carp | wells and cisterns | holds deep |
| 6 | mullet | salt lagoons | line-shy |
| 7 | ice char | mountain tarns | cut your own hole, which freezes over; jumps |
| 8 | lightfin | crater lakes, moonlit pools | follows a lantern; its tells show only in your light |
| 9 | hushfin | one shore, during its event | only during the hushfin event; no tells |
| 10 | sturgeon | the same shore | only during the event; every move, slow and long |

**The hushfin event:** every few hours the sea draws back along one shore, announced to everyone a
few minutes ahead, and for about 15 minutes the top fish can be caught there.
