# Ranged

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 3.4, Artifice, and `docs/systems.md` (calibration, a
proposal). Not built.*

## Summary

The style of the spine's surface people (still called the Rationalists in the game): patient,
precise, from a distance. Slings, crossbows, bows and thrown weapons from Artifice. Trained by ranged
damage.

## Goals

- A style that wants you to plant your feet and keep a line of sight ([pillar 4](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).
- Ammunition the player makes themselves ([pillar 1](../../1-overview/pillars.md)); slings throw mined
  stone, so they cost nothing to run.

## Ties to the setting

The surface people refuse to serve any god; they measure, calculate and shoot from far off. Its
higher tiers use moon-touched materials, tying it to the cracked moon they watch.

## How it is trained

Ranged damage. One shot uses one round of ammunition; a third can be picked up again at the target.

**Its engine is tipped ammunition:** tips from other skills' materials (first, Mining's humour gems)
with effects that trigger on a shot. Designed in outline only.

**Calibration** (a proposal): a meter that builds while you keep shooting one target from one tile
and gives accuracy and critical chance; a miss, a step or a new target costs it.

## The ladder

Weapons and ammunition by tier, from Artifice.

## Ties to other skills

Artifice (weapons, ammunition), Smithing (heads), Woodcutting (shafts), Butchering (feathers,
sinew), Tailoring (leathers), Mining (stone, gem tips).
