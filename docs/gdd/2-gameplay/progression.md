# Progression

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/skills.md` 2.1, 2.2, 2.5, 3.1 and `docs/areas/rules.md` 2.*

A player grows by training sixteen skills from 1 to 100, each up a ladder of ten tiers, and by
finishing quests that open new places and stories. There are no character classes and no level bands
on the land: a player can try anything, and requirements, not zones, decide what they can do.

## Levels

- **Every skill runs from 1 to 100** on RuneScape's experience curve; experience keeps counting past
  100 for its own sake (to 200 million).
- **Pace** (the owner, 2026-11-03): it varies from skill to skill and is neither too fast nor
  mind-numbingly slow. **Reaching the next tier should feel like an accomplishment, with time to
  explore it before you outlevel it**, so every level bracket needs something exciting to reach. The
  end game is denser, because most time is spent there, but the way there must be fun.
- **The first tuning target:** about 50 hours of focused play from 1 to 100 in a skill, front-loaded
  (about 5 hours to level 40, about 37 to level 90). AFK methods earn about half the target rate;
  dangerous or attentive ones up to one and a half times it.
- **Level 100** in a skill earns its cape. Nothing else waits at the top.

## Tiers

Every gathering and making skill climbs **the same ten tiers, one every ten levels**, so a tier-5
pickaxe, ore, bar and sword all ask for level 40.

- **Tiers belong to land, not to the build order.** Each tier's resource is most at home in one kind
  of land and turns up thinly elsewhere, so every region is great for one or two skills, ordinary for
  the rest, and never made obsolete by a newer one.
- **Resources aren't rare; drops are.** A resource can be scarce (few nodes, slow, dangerous), but
  never a lucky roll.
- **Special equipment sits outside the ladder:** each piece designed on its own, usually a boss drop,
  a demanding process, sometimes a special place (the great forge at Frostpeak Forge).
- Tier 10 is an ordinary tier, only harder.

## Requirements

**Requirements are hard limits.** Without the level you can't do it at all; a level never becomes a
chance to fail. The only way past one is a **boost** (a draught, a food, a hymn) that raises your
level for a while.
- Weapons need their style's skill; armour needs Defence; tools need their gathering skill.
- Making an item needs the making skill at its tier, never the skill that wears it.

## Combat level

One number for how strong a fighter is, from 1 to 100: mostly the best of Melee, Ranged and
Occultism, with Defence and half of Religion. Creatures show their own level. It is a guide, not a
lock: anything can be attacked.

## Stages of an account

Used to check that the world has something for everyone, never as a gate:

| Stage | Tiers | Quests |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Early** | 1 to 3 | novice, intermediate |
| **Mid** | 4 to 6 | experienced |
| **Late** | 7 to 10 | master, grandmaster |

Every area should have reasons to come at more than one stage ([pillar 3](../1-overview/pillars.md)).
