# The humours

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/systems.md` and `docs/lore.md` I.*

## Summary

Bodies in R'th run on the four humours of old medicine: blood, phlegm, and yellow and black bile.
They are nature, not cosmology, and they are what a fight is made of. Every player has all of them,
whatever they fight with. Devotion sits beside them, for the church's hymns.

## Goals

- Give R'th's combat its identity: resources from the body, not mana and rage ([pillar 4](../1-overview/pillars.md)).
- One resource every style shares (phlegm), and engines of each style's own on top.

## Ties to the setting

The humours are how bodies work in this world; the peoples practise arts built on them. Occultists
work bile; the church takes blood; medicine purges and balances. Gems each carry a humour, and fish
are phlegmatic.

## How it works

| Resource | What it is | Fills | Empties |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Blood** | Your life. | Food; resting out of combat; a slow healing draught | Damage; some verses cost it |
| **Phlegm** | Adrenaline, every style. 0 to 100. | Basic abilities build it | Strong abilities spend it; it fades out of combat |
| **Bile** | Occultism's axis, from black (-100) to yellow (+100). | Occult abilities push it either way | Settles back towards 0 out of combat |
| **Devotion** | The church's hymns. Ten per Religion level. | Praying at an altar; draughts; waking after death | Each hymn sung drains it; it never comes back by itself |

**Bile's two poles.** Leaning yellow (choleric) makes you hit harder and take more; leaning black
(melancholic) makes you take less and hit softer. The effect grows the further you lean, and some
abilities need a lean before they can be used. Nothing else: no extremes that punish.

**Max blood** comes from Defence.

## What the player sees

The main action bar shows blood, phlegm and devotion as three gauges, and the bile axis as two halves
filling outward from a black centre. Bile's buff and debuff show in the buff bars.

## Odd cases

- After about six seconds out of combat, bile drifts home, phlegm fades and blood starts to return.
- Death wakes you with bile at 0.
