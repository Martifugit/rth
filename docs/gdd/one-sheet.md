# The one-sheet

*Draft (2026-11-03). The whole game on one page (Scott Rogers' one-sheet). Every line comes from a
decision already made; where nothing is decided yet it says so.*

- **Title:** R'th (working title, and the world's name).
- **Genre:** an online role-playing game: a skill-based sandbox with ability combat, played on a tile
  grid in fixed 0.6-second ticks.
- **Players:** an invited group of friends, at most about 200, on one private server. Not sold.
- **The game in two sentences:** You wake on a bier in a small river town, a stranger the church's
  bell tolled for the night before, in a world that was made but never finished and is starting to
  change again. You train sixteen skills, fight with the body's own humours, and walk out across a
  hand-made continent of rival powers that all want something from you.
- **The player:** an unaligned stranger with no past anyone knows. They can swear to the empire,
  learn from the occult houses, study with the spine's surface people and trade with the dwarves, and
  most will do some of each ([lore, VI](3-story/back-story.md)).
- **Pillars:** candidates, waiting on the owner: see [pillars](1-overview/pillars.md).
- **What makes it R'th:**
  1. **Combat from the humours.** Blood is your life, phlegm your adrenaline, and each style has its
     own engine on top: occultists swing a bile axis between hot yellow and cold black, melee
     knights read blood-paid verses that reward fighting low, and every style sings the church's
     hymns.
  2. **A world in revision.** Places appear and turn up moved; old maps are true only in the
     Dreaming; things leak back from the heap of what the world threw away. The game starts at the
     turn, so new content arrives as part of the story.
  3. **Self-sufficient skilling.** Sixteen skills, each a pipeline one player can run alone, with a
     relaxed active layer that works with the place (fire-setting a rock face, tending a coppice,
     keeping a forge roaring), and the carcass at the centre: killing things is how most materials
     enter the world.
- **Games it is like, and how it differs:**
  - *RuneScape 3*: the feel of play (abilities on a global cooldown, adrenaline, long skilling
    loops, quests and bosses, a sandbox with no forced faction). R'th differs in its humour
    resources, fewer skills that all feed combat, and no player market to lean on.
  - *Old School RuneScape*: the look (low-poly models, chunky flat textures, the classic layout),
    with a modern dark-fantasy interface.
  - All content is R'th's own: nothing of RuneScape's names, items, quests, creatures or text.
