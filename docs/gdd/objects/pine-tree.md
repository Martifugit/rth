# Pine Tree

## What it is

A tall evergreen that grows on high, rocky ground. It is one of the [trees](../groups/trees.md).

## Intent

The pine is the tree of the uplands. Players who climb out of the lowlands meet a different wood
there, so [Woodcutting](../skills/woodcutting.md) changes with the land and not only with the level.

## Appearance

Narrow and layered, a dark green cone on a straight bare trunk, taller than a player by two or three
heights. Two colors: needle green and a reddish-brown bark. Needles cover the ground beneath it.
