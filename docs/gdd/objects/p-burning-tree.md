# P-burning-tree

## What it is

A tree that always burns and grows very fast, spreading fire around it. One sprouts somewhere in the
world now and then, and one stands at the end of every [Wildfires](../activities/wildfires.md) run.

## Intent

It's why wildfires happen, and why [fire brigades](../groups/fire-brigades.md) fight them all the
time. It's also a mystery players should wonder about.
