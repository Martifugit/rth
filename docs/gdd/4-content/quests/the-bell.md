# The Bell

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/quests/the-bell.md` (a skeleton, 2026-10-27) and `docs/lore.md` VI
(the premise is canon). Working title. Not built.*

## Synopsis

The night before the game begins, Tolling's bell rang for a death: fishers had pulled a stranger out
of Wennow, and the church laid the body out in the dead-house. In the morning the stranger got up: the
player. The quest is there for whoever asks what happened; the town keeps nudging (the bell-ringer
looks twice, the ledger says dead, Edda says too much).

## Objectives

- Tell the player who they are in this world, as far as Tolling knows: a stranger the bell tolled for.
- Teach the first systems, one a step: talking and the journal, the bank, consumables and shops,
  walking out, the first fight.
- Introduce the town's people and three threads: the church (the ledger), the occult (Corvin's
  curiosity), the Dreaming (Edda); point at the ferryman.
- Pillar 2: **it ends with more questions than it started with.**
- **Requirements:** none. **Rewards:** not set; Dreamless opens.

## Introductory material

There is no opening scene. New players appear in the dead-house and can walk straight out and go
mining. The bell-ringer at the foot of the stairs starts it.

## Critical path

| # | Where | Who | What happens | Teaches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The dead-house | the bell-ringer | He finds the "body" up and about, and sends the player to the clerk to set it right. | talking, the journal |
| 2 | The church house | Clerk Ambrose | The ledger has the player down as dead; what was found on the body went into the strongbox. | the bank: the first withdrawal is your own belongings |
| 3 | The apothecary | Corvin Sallow | He looks the player over, far more curious than an apothecary should be, and sells the first potions. | consumables, shops |
| 4 | Wennow's south shore | the fishers | Where the player was pulled out. Rats on the way; something left at the spot (an idea: an obol, the ferryman's fee, never spent). | walking out, the first fight |
| 5 | The pond | Edda Marsh | Asleep on her feet, she says something only the ferryman would know. | leads into Dreamless |
| 6 | The church | Clerk Ambrose, the bell-ringer | The entry is struck out. The bell-ringer: the bell has never been wrong before. | |

## Closing material

- **The bell's twin:** the bell-ringer mentions that Tolling's bell was cast as one of a pair, and the
  other hangs in a belfry now drowned in Knellmere: the first pull east.
- **Pennick** already knows the player: his conversation at the first death gets a line for it.
- **Someone comes looking,** later and elsewhere: someone who knows a little more than the player and
  gives it out in pieces.

## Sealed

Why the bell rang for the player and why they came back (connected to the revision). The quest must
not answer it.
