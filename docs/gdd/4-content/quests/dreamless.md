# Dreamless

*Draft (2026-11-03). Built (Milestone 6). From the quest's content and `docs/lore.md` II, V.*

## Synopsis

Edda Marsh walks by the pond in her sleep. She says she goes somewhere grey at night, full of threads
and someone counting, and she wants it to stop. Corvin Sallow can brew a dreamless draught, and he is
very interested in where Edda goes at night.

## Objectives

- Introduce Medicine and Corvin's curiosity; touch the Dreaming and the nightmare thread
  ([pillar 2](../../1-overview/pillars.md)).
- Requirements: none. Rewards: experience and items (in data).
