# The dead-house, the crypt and Corvin's cellar

*Draft (2026-11-03). From `docs/areas/tolling-bell-tower.md` (the undercroft decided with the owner,
2026-10-28: a cool vault below the tower; the stone is the soft coursed fieldstone) and

## Synopsis

Three small places under Tolling, each its own little region reached by a stair or a trapdoor: the
dead-house where every player wakes, the church's crypt where The Bell ends, and Corvin's hidden
cellar.

## The dead-house

- **Way in:** the stair down from the bell tower's hall. Every new character starts here.
- **What it is:** a low vault on squat piers in the tower's stone, about 8 by 10 tiles, larger than
  the tower because it serves the whole churchyard. A short passage to the stair, light falling down
  it.
- **Inside:** three or four stone biers in a row: the player's, its shroud thrown back; the others
  shrouded and still. Candles in niches and at the biers' heads. A ledger on a lectern by the stair:
  who lies here (the player's name is struck through later in The Bell).
- **Light:** cold blue-grey, near-black past a few tiles, warm candlelight in pools.
- **Not a door to the realm of the dead** (decided 2026-11-03). Death wakes players on Pennick's
  landing, never here.

## The crypt (proposed)

- **Way in:** a trapdoor in the chapel's floor, opened in The Bell.
- **Inside:** stairs into a passage; an ossuary where Wennow's drowned lie, named; the ledger chest;
  a blocked arch at the far end, kept for an older vault.
- **For:** The Bell's end; later quests.

## Corvin's cellar (proposed)

- **Way in:** a trapdoor behind his counter, after Dreamless.
- **Inside:** his still, shelves of jars, a locked chest.
- **For:** his secret, and the start of his quest line; Occultism's first rites.
