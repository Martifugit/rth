# IV. Areas, dungeons and quests

*Draft.* The game's levels: each place and quest designed in full, to scale, before it is built.
(Baldwin section IV; Ryan's level design.) An area gets its paper design here only when it is next
to be built; until then it is a seed in [III, areas](../3-story/index.md).

| Folder | Holds | Template |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `areas/` | An area's paper design: synopsis, objectives, physical description, map, critical path, encounters, walkthrough. One folder per area, with its dungeons beside it. | [area](../templates/area.md), [dungeon](../templates/dungeon.md) |
| `quests/` | Each quest: premise, steps, critical path, what it needs and changes. | [quest](../templates/quest.md) |
| `activities/` | Bosses, trials, minigames. | [activity](../templates/activity.md) |
