## Synopsis

A repeatable, instanced skilling boss for Religion. A martyr of p-sworn's church lies in a crypt
beneath one of its monuments, and he won't stay at rest. You give blood at the crypt's founts, carry the
phials to his altar to sing his **Requiem**, and survive his shouts until two cleanses lay him back down.
Fail too many mechanics and he wakes, which ends the run.

## Objectives

- Religion's boss: the work is the skill (empowering), and mechanics interrupt it, never replace it.
- Room to get good: routing, pre-stocking, reading tells, choosing how greedy to be.
- Self-contained: you bring food and devotion; nothing depends on other players.

## Introductory material

A sealed crypt door at a monument. Each run is your own copy of the crypt. The lowest tier needs all
three overhead hymns (Religion 41).

## How it plays

- **The loop:** fill blood phials at founts (far founts give more), pour them at the altar to sing the
  Requiem, cleanse at half and at full. Phials don't stack and share your pack with food.
- **The Wake bar** fills slowly by itself and jumps at each failed mechanic; full, the run fails. A clean
  run takes about three minutes.
- **Owed blood:** about once a minute he marks you; pour phials into the offertory within the window.
- **Shouts,** one at a time, from a pool, the gaps shrinking:
  - *Pay what you owe:* stand on a lit sigil in time (or pay in phials).
  - *Hold your tongue:* stop all skilling for a few seconds.
  - *Are you a believer?:* four symbols light in sequence; switch to the matching overhead as each
    projectile lands, and it is blocked completely.
  - *Let there be light:* some founts glow and give more the longer you stay, until it scorches you.

## Rewards

Pledges, a chance at a verse codex, a chest of materials for other skills, a pet, cosmetics,
collection entries. Payout scales with the Wake bar left.
