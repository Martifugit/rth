# The long way back

*Draft (2026-11-03). Built (D3, 2026-11-02). From `docs/lore-rework/death.md`.*

## Synopsis

Instead of paying Pennick, a player can walk back through Lowe: a short, brutal route that is just as
hard for the strongest as for the weakest. At its end the walker gives back everything Pennick holds,
free.

## Objectives

- A free way back from every death ([pillar 1](../../1-overview/pillars.md)), costly in skill instead of
  coin.
- Equal for everyone: its damage scales with your own max blood, so a high level gives no edge.

## Introductory material

The leaning stones on the Landing's south shore open the way, only for someone Pennick holds something
for, and only empty-handed (the grave chest is right there). Anyone else is turned back.

## How it plays

- A causeway through still black water in four stretches, about two and a half minutes at walking pace.
  You can't run: the cold is in your legs.
- **The cold** drains your blood steadily, and blood doesn't return by itself while you walk.
- **Shades** drift on set paths; come too close and one follows a little way and strikes hard.
- **Grasping hands,** unseen until they take you, hold you a few seconds and hurt; thickest on the
  short way through the stone field. The long way round the field has none, but has a shade.
- **Four soul lanterns,** one between each pair of stretches, each warm you once an attempt.
- **Failing:** dying on the way wakes you on the Landing again; Pennick's hold is untouched and his
  clock keeps running. Walk again as often as you like.
- **Pace:** a bot that knows the way arrives with about a fifth of its blood. A first walk, not knowing
  where the hands are, should usually fail.

## Rewards

Everything Pennick holds, free, and you return as if the fee were paid.
