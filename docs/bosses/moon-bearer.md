# The Moon-Bearer (design draft, 2026-11-01)

*A world boss of the cracked moon thread (`docs/lore.md` V). Draft for the owner: nothing here is
decided until passed. The silhouette blockouts in `tools/art/blender/boards/moonbearer_study.py` were
rejected as childish; this is the design work that should have come first.*

## The idea in one line

The arena is the inside of a hollow moon fragment, and the fragment is a hermit crab's shell: the crab
lives in its walls and surfaces through the cracks. The one thing that stands out: **you are fighting
inside its shell.**

## What it is, as a real animal made slightly fantastical

A hermit crab: its soft, curled abdomen stays hidden in the shell (here, in the walls); only the hard
front ever comes out: the head shield, the eyes, the claws and the walking legs. Made fantastical by:
- **scale** (its front shield about as wide as a cottage),
- **the light**: it has lived in the dark inside the moon so long it has turned pale and faintly
  translucent, its own cold glow showing through the cracks (the player's clue where it will surface),
- **the walls' rock on its legs**: lying still along a crack, a leg passes for part of the wall.

## Anatomy to build from (real crab anatomy, not tubes)

Every limb of a crab is the same chain of hard segments joined by hinges, each hinge bending in one
plane, the joint membrane between them soft and darker:

| Segment | Walking leg | Claw (cheliped) |
|---|---|---|
| coxa, basis-ischium | short, hidden under the shield's edge | short |
| **merus** | the longest: flattened, a ridge or row of spines on its top edge | long, heavy, triangular in section |
| carpus | short, the "knee" | short, knobbed: the wrist |
| propodus | long, slimmer | **the palm**: swollen, ridged, ending in the fixed finger |
| dactyl | a curved, tapering point | the movable finger, closing on the fixed one; both toothed |

- **The front shield (carapace front)**: a broad, flattened shield wider than it is long, its front edge
  between the eyes drawn into a short serrated brow (rostrum), its surface divided into raised regions
  (gastric, branchial) like plates of armour, edged with a row of blunt spines.
- **The eyes**: on thick stalks in sockets at the front edge, with a dark, faceted eye at the tip and a
  horn above it (as the ghost crab's); they fold down into grooves along the brow.
- **The claws**: lopsided, as hermit crabs are. The great claw's palm is the creature's heaviest, most
  sculpted shape: ridges, tubercles and a toothed cutting edge.
- **The legs**: long and thin (a spider crab's), each segment a faceted section, not a round tube; spines
  along the merus; the dactyls curved points that hook into the cracks' edges.

## Proportions (from the front shield's width W)

W about 4 m. Great claw: merus 0.6 W, palm 0.75 W long, 0.4 W deep. Small claw: about half. Walking legs:
merus 1.1 W, carpus 0.3 W, propodus 0.8 W, dactyl 0.5 W, so a leg reaches about 2.7 W (about 11 m). Eye
stalks 0.35 W. The front shield and claws together are what comes out of the main crack; the legs come out
of the others.

## Silhouette at the game camera

A broad, low, spined shield in a glowing crack, two heavy eyes raised on stalks, one huge claw lowered in
front like a shield of its own, and long hooked legs reaching out of the cracks round the room.

## How it gets built (by stages, each passed before the next)

1. **One limb study**: a walking leg and the great claw alone, segment by segment from the table above,
   with real hinge joints (segments overlapping at a hinge, the darker joint membrane between), faceted
   sections, spines; judged up close and at the game camera.
2. **The front shield and eyes**: the shield's regions and spined edge, the eyestalks in their sockets.
3. **Assembly** coming out of one crack; the wall and the light.
4. Only then the rock crust on the legs and the glow.
