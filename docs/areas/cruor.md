## The brief

**For:** the capital, early to late: every power's road passes it. Arriving should feel like
reaching the centre of the world.

**Who:** the empire and p-sworn's church; everyone passing through.

**Shape:** **a hill in a river's loop.** The great river comes from the north-west, the lake's
outflow from the south-west; they meet below the hill and the river loops round it on three sides
before running east. Wide open ground all round: fields, the monument road, gardens. The city is
the skyline from the whole heartland.

**Ground:** grass and meadow, fields (wheat, ley, furrow) round the city, heath and bracken east,
forest edge north, river water; overlays: the gravel paths in, bridges.

**Landmarks and views:** Cruor's hill and the cathedral at its crown, seen from everywhere in the
heartland (keep every road's last approach open towards it).

**Routes:** Palmerway from the lake; the north road (to p-greatbridge and Meridian); the east road
(to the enclave); the forest road (to p-forestgate); the Gall road (south past p-weir). Bridges
where each crosses the great river or the outflow.

**Places:** Cruor (city); sites s-lake-monument (a blood monument on the road in), s-undercroft
(the undercroft below the hill, mid game).

**Counts** (skeleton): little to gather round a capital; farmland and its creatures; the city's
people come at stage 3.
