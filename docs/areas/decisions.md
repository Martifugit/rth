# The area plan: decisions and parking lot

## Decisions

World-wide choices only: moving a range, or dropping or adding a target. A single place that
differs from the norm needs no entry: its content is the design.

| Date | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-22 | The world plays as a RuneScape-like sandbox: no level bands. Areas stay relevant through reasons to visit (quests, skilling, NPCs, activities, travel) across early, mid and late stages. | Owner. |
| 2026-10-22 | Targets are ranges for the world or a realm, never quotas for a place. | Owner: averages applied everywhere would make a monotone world; a city without a bank can be the point. |
| 2026-10-22 | Areas are described by their content, not by character labels. Big places hold parts of their own (districts, levels, caves), and content can depend on a state (quest progress, events). | Owner: a one-word role is too thin for a city with several districts that changes over time. |
| 2026-10-22 | Traps and gates are allowed when deliberate: committed content (boss instances, quest points of no return, dangerous zones) is marked and entered by choice; resources may sit behind quests. What's never allowed is a dead end: an uncompletable requirement chain, or a challenge whose supplies a player who reached it can't make for themselves. | Owner. |
| 2026-10-22 | Cruor is the one very large city. The Candora range is huge and vertical, rich in caves, dungeons and underground levels. | Owner. |
| 2026-10-22 | The world sketch (`images/map.png`) is a strong concept, not a fixed layout. | Owner. |
| 2026-10-22 | New places are named `p-{suggestedName}` until the owner names them. Quest design is deferred until the world exists to put quests in. | Owner. |
| 2026-10-22 | Pass 1 scale: 1.75 tiles per sketch pixel (about 1.0M tiles of land, Tolling to Cruor about 200 tiles), one constant in `skeleton.json`. | Proposed; awaiting the owner's confirmation. |
| 2026-10-22 | Pass 1 layout: Tolling sits inland from the bay (Wennow is a mere); Meridian is at Candora's south-western end; the Escapement is in Scalth and the Mainspring Pit in Swallow; the Veilwood lies by Selenfall and the moonfall scar and holds ways into the Dreaming, a realm of its own off the map. | Owner, pass 1. |
