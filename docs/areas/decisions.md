# The area plan: decisions and parking lot

## Decisions

World-wide choices only: moving a range, dropping a target, adding a character. A single place
that differs from the norm needs only its character (`rules.md`, principle 2).

| Date | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-22 | The world plays as a RuneScape-like sandbox: no level bands. Areas stay relevant through reasons to visit (quests, skilling, NPCs, activities, travel) across early, mid and late stages. | Owner. |
| 2026-10-22 | Targets are ranges for the world or a realm, never quotas for a place. Each place declares a character, and its services follow from it. | Owner: averages applied everywhere would make a monotone world; a city without a bank can be the point. |
| 2026-10-22 | Cruor is the one very large city. The Candora range is huge and vertical, rich in caves, dungeons and underground levels. | Owner. |
| 2026-10-22 | The world sketch (`images/map.png`) is a strong concept, not a fixed layout. | Owner. |
| 2026-10-22 | New places are named `p-{suggestedName}` until the owner names them. Quest design is deferred until the world exists to put quests in. | Owner. |
