# Creatures of R'th (draft for discussion, 2026-11-01)

*A wide brainstorm of the creature families R'th could hold, drawn from myth, folklore, legend and
fantastic stories the world over. Nothing here is decided. It is a menu to choose from, area by area.*

How to read it:
- **Families** are kinds of creature, each with the myths it grows from, how it could belong to R'th,
  and how it would be built (the body plans at the end, `tools/art/blender/creatures.md`).
- **The lore's rule applies:** a creature grows from a myth reimagined, so the inspiration stays
  discoverable but the creature is R'th's own. Its look comes from the world (the humours, the
  revision, the cracked moon, the deep, blood oaths), not from any one picture of the myth.
- **Boundaries:** myth and folklore are everyone's. Creatures that belong to one game or one
  publisher (RuneScape's, and things like the beholder or mind flayer) are out, and so are their names.
- **Butchering's six families** (`docs/skills.md` 5: wildlife, Scalth-touched, occult-made, war-beasts,
  moonborn, the deep) say where creatures live and what they yield. The families here say what a
  creature *is*; each would fall into one of those six, or be a boss or a power (marked).

## Families

### 1. Dragon-kin
*Myth:* the dragons of Europe (Fafnir, the Lambton Worm, the lindworm), the many-headed Slavic
zmey, Tiamat, Ladon, the Chinese long, the feathered serpent of Mesoamerica, Jörmungandr.
*In R'th:* the true dragons as **old beasts**, few and named; their lesser kin out in the world:
**drakes** (wingless stalkers, war-beasts), **wyverns** (two-legged, barbed), **wyrms** and
**lindworms** (legless or two-legged worms of the deep), **amphipteres** (winged serpents), sea
serpents. *Built:* dragon, drake, wyvern.

### 2. Serpents and the serpent-born
*Myth:* the basilisk and cockatrice (death by gaze), the amphisbaena (a head at each end), the
hydra, the naga, the lamia, the Rainbow Serpent, the Mongolian death worm.
*In R'th:* a great snake with a head at each end in Sere's dunes; a gaze that turns flesh to salt;
a hydra whose heads regrow from bile. *Body:* serpent.

### 3. Many-headed beasts
*Myth:* the Lernaean hydra, Yamata no Orochi (eight heads, eight tails), Cerberus and Orthrus,
the three-headed zmey.
*In R'th:* bosses. A guardian of the landing's far shore for the long way back; a beast whose heads
each speak in a different voice (the betrayers?). *Built:* Cerberus (early).

### 4. Composite beasts
*Myth:* the chimera, manticore, griffin, hippogriff, sphinx, lamassu, the Egyptian Ammit (crocodile,
lion and hippo, who eats the hearts of the unworthy dead), the bake-kujira.
*In R'th:* two natural homes: **occult-made** grafts (Gall's houses joining beasts), and **the
rejected**, wrong combinations from p-spoil. A lamassu-like guardian at Sere's wells; something like
Ammit on the landing. *Body:* mammal or mixed.

### 5. Great birds
*Myth:* the roc, the thunderbird, the phoenix, the simurgh, garuda, harpies, the Stymphalian birds
(bronze feathers), the caladrius (a bird that takes sickness away), the ziz.
*In R'th:* a roc nesting high on the spine; harpies at the passes taxing travellers like the
surface people do; a fire-bird of Gall that rises from its ash; a caladrius for Medicine's lore.
*Body:* bird (wing kit, feathered panels still to come).

### 6. Water: rivers, lakes and fens
*Myth:* the kelpie and each-uisge (water horses that drown their riders), Jenny Greenteeth and the
grindylow (river hags that drag children under), the kappa, the vodyanoy and rusalka, the bunyip,
the nixie, will-o'-the-wisps over bogs.
*In R'th:* Tolling's river Wennow, where the player was pulled out, is a natural home. A water
horse in the great lake; green-toothed things in the fens of the lesser houses. *Body:* mammal,
humanoid, amphibian.

### 7. Sea and the drowned
*Myth:* the kraken, leviathan, Charybdis, sirens and merfolk, selkies, the ningyo, the jellyfish
lights of sailors' tales, ghost ships.
*In R'th:* the southern and eastern islands, and the coasts; a leviathan as an old beast; drowned
sailors who answer to the ferryman's rival waters. *Body:* fish, cephalopod (tentacles: a new kit).

### 8. The restless dead
*Myth:* the draugr and revenant, wights and barrow-wights, ghouls, the banshee, the dullahan
(headless rider), the jiangshi (hopping dead), the vetala, mummies, corpse-candles.
*In R'th:* the dead stay dead, so the ones that don't are news: oath-breakers whose blood was called
in, martyrs who won't rest (the Unquiet Saint), the dead met on the long way back. *Body:* humanoid
(player-body variants), skeletal.

### 9. Blood-drinkers
*Myth:* the vampire, strigoi and upir, the aswang and penanggalan (a head that flies off trailing its
organs), the chupacabra, the vampire bat.
*In R'th:* a perfect fit for p-sworn's blood: things drawn to the monuments' gathered blood, a
debt-collector of broken oaths, bats in the crypts beneath Cruor. *Body:* humanoid, bat, small beast.

### 10. Shapeshifters and borrowed faces
*Myth:* werewolves and berserkers, the skinwalker, the kitsune and huli jing (fox spirits), the
selkie's skin, the wendigo, the rakshasa, the púca.
*In R'th:* the occultists' transformation arts ("slowly strange") give werebeasts; the Faceless and
their gods wear borrowed faces, so a creature that wears someone else's could be their servant.
*Body:* mammal and humanoid in two forms.

### 11. Fae and tricksters
*Myth:* the fair folk and changelings, redcaps, the Wild Hunt, brownies and boggarts, the tengu,
tanuki and kappa tricksters, Anansi.
*In R'th:* the Dreaming is their natural home: beings half in dreams, stealing sleepers away; the
Wild Hunt as a revision event riding out over the land. *Body:* humanoid, small beast.

### 12. Giants
*Myth:* the jötnar (frost and fire giants), cyclopes, the Fomorians, trolls, ogres, oni, the titans,
the Nephilim, the giants who built the old walls.
*In R'th:* the face in the cliff hints that giants once were (sealed); trolls of the spine's high
passes; ruins "of a style nobody made" built at a giant's scale; frost giants in the cold north.
*Body:* humanoid, scaled up and heavy.

### 13. Little folk and mine spirits
*Myth:* knockers and tommyknockers (mine spirits who knock to warn of collapse), kobolds, duergar,
gnomes, goblins, the domovoi.
*In R'th:* knockers in the dwarves' mines, warning or misleading; the older peoples of Undermost
seen only as glimpses; goblin-like scavengers among the rejected. *Body:* humanoid, small.

### 14. Constructs and the animated
*Myth:* the golem, Talos (the bronze guardian of Crete), homunculi, ushabti, gargoyles, living
statues, scarecrows, the automata of legend.
*In R'th:* the church's monuments and statues (the straw penitent already exists); occult
homunculi; clockwork from the surface people's observatories; dwarven seal-wardens that keep the
deep shut. *Body:* constructs (rigid parts: a new kit, but an easy one).

### 15. Plant and forest beings
*Myth:* the green man and leshy, treants and wood-wives, dryads, the mandrake (whose scream kills),
the woodwose, the kodama, man-eating plants, fungus-folk.
*In R'th:* Venaria's forests; moonborn strange growth where fragments fall; mandrakes for Foraging
and Medicine lore. *Body:* trunk-and-limb (the tree kit), fungal.

### 16. Elementals and spirits of place
*Myth:* salamanders, undines and sylphs, the djinn and ifrit, will-o'-the-wisps, the genius loci,
the kami, ash and smoke spirits.
*In R'th:* fire salamanders in Gall's volcanoes (and its occult fire); a djinn-like spirit in
each of Sere's deep wells, which explains why only Sere knows the water; spirits of revision-moved
places that don't know where they are. *Body:* amorphous (open), salamander (lizard).

### 17. Demons and the abominable
*Myth:* imps, incubi and succubi, the asura and daeva, the oni, the rakshasa, the shedim.
*In R'th:* nothing called a demon (the world has gods and powers, not a hell), but the betrayers'
fused abomination and the beings they wear are the same idea; the occult houses' worst summonings.
*Body:* humanoid and mixed.

### 18. Hounds and omens of death
*Myth:* the black dog (barghest, Black Shuck, the gytrash, the church grim), the hellhound, the
cù-sìth, Garmr at the gate of the dead.
*In R'th:* hounds of the realm of the dead seen at crossroads before a death; a church grim that
haunts the churchyard at Tolling's bell tower. *Body:* mammal (the wolf rebuild).

### 19. Great wild beasts and the primeval
*Myth:* the Nemean lion, the Calydonian boar, the white stag and the Ceryneian hind, the Questing
Beast, the aurochs, the behemoth, the yeti and bigfoot, the great bear.
*In R'th:* old beasts, one of a kind, hunted in legends and quests; a white stag that only appears
in places that have moved; animals that came back with the revision after a thousand years.
*Body:* mammal.

### 20. Horned and hoofed wonders
*Myth:* the unicorn, qilin and karkadann, the catoblepas (whose gaze kills; it can barely lift its
head), the bonnacon, the peryton (a stag-bird with a man's shadow), the al-mi'raj (a horned hare),
the wolpertinger.
*In R'th:* wonders for wildlife and the Dreaming; a peryton whose shadow is the shadow of the last
person it killed, for the Faceless thread. *Body:* mammal.

### 21. Nightmares and dream-eaters
*Myth:* the mara (who sits on sleepers' chests), the alp, the baku (who eats bad dreams), the
sandman, the bogeyman, the hag of sleep paralysis.
*In R'th:* the nightmare thread itself; a baku-like creature that hunts the nightmare and could be
an ally; dream-eaters for the Dreaming skill. *Body:* mammal, humanoid, amorphous.

### 22. The cold north
*Myth:* the wendigo, the yuki-onna (snow woman), the frost giants, ice wyrms, the yeti.
*In R'th:* the northern frontier and Frostpeak; the wendigo's hunger as a curse on those who break
their oaths in the cold. *Body:* humanoid, mammal, serpent.

### 23. The desert
*Myth:* the ghūl of the Arabian desert, the djinn, the sandworm and death worm, the manticore,
scorpion-men (the girtablilu who guard the sun's gate), the lamassu.
*In R'th:* Sere: scorpion-folk guarding the oldest wells; the ghūl that leads caravans astray from
their water. *Body:* arthropod, humanoid, serpent.

### 24. Giant vermin and swarms
*Myth:* the jorōgumo and tsuchigumo (great spiders), Arachne, the myrmecoleon (ant-lion), plague
swarms of locusts, the Myrmidons.
*In R'th:* Scalth-touched vermin (the vitriol mite); swarms that arrive with a revision; giant
spiders in the deep. *Body:* arthropod.

### 25. The moonborn
*Myth:* the moon rabbit, the moon's hare and toad, lunar madness, star-fallen things, the
Selenites of old tales.
*In R'th:* the cracked moon's own: pale, crystalline, too many joints, growing out of the craters;
a world boss for the largest falls. Mostly invented, which suits a thread that is R'th's own.
*Body:* anything with crystal growths.

### 26. The deep and the bound
*Myth:* the things beneath the world: Typhon under Etna, Fenrir bound until the end, Apep beneath
the sun's path, the old ones of weird fiction (in spirit, not by name).
*In R'th:* what the dwarves' seals hold, and everything that lives near it: blind crawlers, deep
wyrms, things that grow toward a warmth that isn't the sun. *Body:* serpent, arthropod, amorphous.

### 27. The rejected
*Myth:* the failed first creations: the Popol Vuh's people of mud (who melted) and of wood (who had
no souls), the Greek ages of man, Plato's split beings, the unformed things before order in many
creation stories.
*In R'th:* the most R'th creatures of all. Drafts of species that were removed: a deer with no face,
a bird too heavy to fly, a wolf made of the wrong number of parts, a whole flock of something that was
never finished. Flat untextured patches and blank planes suit the low-poly look perfectly. They leak
from p-spoil as the revision stirs. *Body:* any, deliberately incomplete.
