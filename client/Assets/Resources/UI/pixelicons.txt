# R'th pixel icons: original 16x16 pixel art, loaded by IconLibrary at runtime.
# A PNG at Resources/Icons/<id>.png replaces the icon of the same id, so real art can arrive one
# icon at a time. Format: "@ <id>" then 16 rows of 16 characters.
# Palette: . clear, k outline, x black, w white, g grey, G dark grey, y gold, Y dark gold,
# r red, R dark red, b brown, n dark brown, s tan, u blue, U dark blue, e green, E dark green,
# p purple, P dark purple, o orange, c glass.
# B L D are the item's own colour: base, light and dark (tinted per item).
